## 摘要

本文系统考察佛教唯识学（Yogācāra Buddhism）与西方心理学主要传统之间的深层关系，在比较哲学、跨文化心理学与认知科学的多重视角下，对精神分析与深层心理学（弗洛伊德、客体关系、自体心理学、关系精神分析、神经精神分析）、现象学与存在主义心理学（胡塞尔、海德格尔、萨特、罗洛·梅、亚隆、弗兰克尔、格式塔）、行为主义与认知传统（华生、斯金纳、贝克、艾利斯、预测处理理论）、人本主义与超个人心理学（马斯洛、罗杰斯、超个人运动）、发展心理学与依恋理论（皮亚杰、埃里克森、鲍尔比、西格尔的人际神经生物学）、情感与躯体心理学（潘克塞普的情感神经科学、波雷斯的多元迷走神经理论、利文的躯体体验、范德科尔克的创伤研究）、社会与文化心理学、积极心理学与第三波认知行为治疗，以及当代前沿的预测编码、4E认知科学、迷幻剂辅助治疗等共十个维度，分别与唯识学的核心概念体系进行系统性的对照分析。本文论证：两者的对话建立在共同面对人类苦难这一文明根基之上；然而在目的论结构（解脱 vs. 心理健康）、认识论地位（第一人称实证 vs. 第三人称实验）、自我观（无我体证 vs. 自我实现）与实在论预设上存在根本性差异，这些差异既是对话的困难，也是其最深刻的价值所在。本文进一步评估以正念认知疗法、接受与承诺治疗、慈悲聚焦疗法等为代表的第三波整合实践，并在若干创新前沿方向上提出开创性的比较分析，特别是预测处理与阿赖耶识种子机制的深层结构同构、多元迷走神经理论与佛教安全感修行的对话、情感神经科学七大基本情绪系统与唯识烦恼心所的精密对应，以及迷幻剂研究对唯识意识状态理论的意外印证。本文的核心论点是：唯识学对心理过程的分析，在深度、精度与系统性上均超越了现有西方心理学框架，其独特的解脱论维度为心理学提供了一个迄今未被充分开发的理论资源；同时，西方心理学的实证方法与临床经验，也为唯识学的某些主张提供了可操作化的研究路径。真正有价值的对话，需要在充分尊重两个传统各自内在完整性的前提下，在批判性审视与诚实承认差异的基础上进行。

## 第一节：引言——两个人类文明对心灵的独立探索

### 一、问题的起点与时代背景

人类心灵的苦恼，是跨越文明、跨越时代的普遍经验。为什么人会受苦？苦的根源是什么？心灵如何运作？改变的可能性在哪里？这些问题，在西方心理学与东方佛教唯识学这两个完全独立发展的传统中，各自形成了极为精密的回答体系。它们在历史上从未相遇，却在今天以一种奇特的共鸣与碰撞，成为21世纪思想界最富张力的跨文化对话之一。

西方心理学作为现代学科，从1879年威廉·冯特（Wilhelm Wundt）在莱比锡建立第一个实验心理学实验室算起，至今不过一百五十年历史。¹ 然而在这短短一个半世纪内，它已经发展出极为多元的理论传统——弗洛伊德（Freud）的精神分析、荣格（Jung）的分析心理学、华生（Watson）与斯金纳（Skinner）的行为主义、皮亚杰（Piaget）的发展心理学、马斯洛（Maslow）的人本主义、贝克（Beck）的认知疗法、潘克塞普（Panksepp）的情感神经科学、塞利格曼（Seligman）的积极心理学、波雷斯（Porges）的多元迷走神经理论——每一个传统都代表着人类对自身心灵问题的一种深刻探索，也都携带着各自的文化预设与历史局限。

唯识学（Yogācāra Buddhism）作为佛教哲学的精密化发展，以弥勒（Maitreyanātha，约270–350 CE）、无著（Asaṅga，约310–390 CE）、世亲（Vasubandhu，约320–400 CE）为奠基人，以陈那（Dignāga，约480–540 CE）、护法（Dharmapāla，约530–561 CE）为发展者，以玄奘（602–664 CE）的翻译与窥基（632–682 CE）的系统整理为汉传高峰，历经一千七百年的传承与发展，形成了对意识结构与心理过程最为精密的分析传统之一。² 其核心，不是建立一门关于心灵的科学，而是通过对心理过程的如实认识，为有情众生开辟一条从苦恼的根源彻底解脱的道路。

今天，这两个传统的相遇，不再只是学者书房里的比较哲学练习。它发生在医院的治疗室里（正念认知疗法每年拯救数以万计的复发性抑郁患者）、发生在神经科学实验室里（冥想改变大脑结构的实证研究改变了神经可塑性理论）、发生在人工智能研究者的困惑中（意识的难问题使最前沿的技术研究者转向东方哲学）、发生在迷幻剂辅助治疗的临床试验里（致幻剂诱发的神秘体验与佛教禅定描述的惊人相似性引发了新的思考）。这场对话，比任何时候都更迫切，也比任何时候都更需要方法论的严格自觉。

本文的目标，是在批判性地整理现有对话成果的基础上，对两个传统在十个主要维度上的深层关系进行迄今最为系统的哲学分析：既指出真实存在的结构性共鸣，也诚实标注根本性的差异，同时在若干创新前沿方向上提出新的比较视角，为这一领域未来更深入、更严格的研究提供一个方法论自觉的基础。


> **EN对应 · S1.1 The Starting Point and Its Contemporary Urgency**
>
> Why do human beings suffer? What are the sources of mental distress? How does the mind operate? Where does the possibility of change lie? These questions have generated, within two entirely independent intellectual traditions, extraordinarily refined systems of response. Today, these traditions meet — not as a curiosity of comparative scholarship, but as an increasingly urgent practical and theoretical necessity, unfolding in hospital treatment rooms, in neuroscience laboratories, in the perplexities of artificial intelligence researchers, and in the emerging clinical trials of psychedelic-assisted therapy.
>
> Western psychology as a modern academic discipline dates, by conventional reckoning, from Wilhelm Wundt’s establishment of the first experimental psychology laboratory in Leipzig in 1879.¹ Within this relatively brief century and a half, it has generated a strikingly diverse theoretical landscape — Freud’s psychoanalysis, Jung’s analytical psychology, Watson and Skinner’s behaviorism, Piaget’s developmental psychology, Maslow’s humanism, Beck’s cognitive therapy, Panksepp’s affective neuroscience, Seligman’s positive psychology, Porges’ polyvagal theory — each tradition representing a profound exploration of the human mind, each carrying its distinctive cultural presuppositions and historical limitations.
>
> Yogācāra Buddhism, as the systematic philosophical refinement of Buddhist thought, was founded by Maitreyanātha (c. 270–350 CE), Asaṅga (c. 310–390 CE), and Vasubandhu (c. 320–400 CE), developed by Dignāga (c. 480–540 CE) and Dharmapāla (c. 530–561 CE), and reached its Chinese apogee through Xuanzang’s (602–664 CE) monumental translation work and Kuiji’s (632–682 CE) systematic commentarial elaboration.² Across seventeen centuries of transmission and development, it has produced one of the most refined analytical traditions for investigating the structure of consciousness and the mechanisms of psychological process in human intellectual history. Its animating purpose, however, is not to establish a science of the mind but to open, through precise understanding of mental operations, a path of genuine liberation from the sources of suffering.
>
> The encounter between these two traditions has generated significant scholarly momentum since the mid-twentieth century — from Jung’s early engagement with Buddhist texts, through Fromm and Suzuki’s cross-cultural dialogue, Mark Epstein’s clinical integrations, and Jon Kabat-Zinn’s mindfulness-based interventions, to the contemporary explosion of contemplative neuroscience. Yet this encounter has also exposed systematic methodological difficulties and conceptual confusions that deserve more careful philosophical treatment than they have so far received. This paper aims to address that need.
### 二、两个传统的方法论差异：在进入比较之前

在进入具体比较之前，有三个方法论预设需要明确声明，因为它们将贯穿本文的所有分析：

**第一，目的论差异的优先性**。唯识学的终极目标是**解脱**（mokṣa）——从轮回苦恼中彻底解放，具体表现为转依（āśraya-parāvṛtti），即阿赖耶识的根本转化。西方心理学的目标，无论是弗洛伊德的”使无意识成为有意识”、马斯洛的”自我实现”，还是塞利格曼的”蓬勃发展”（flourishing），都是在此世的心理健康与功能改善，不涉及解脱论意义上的存在性转化。这一差异是根本性的，任何忽视它的比较研究都将产生系统性的误读。

**第二，语言类型的不对称**（此处引用本文方法论框架，详见第二节）。佛典语言至少可区分为分析性定义语言、导引性语言与方便说三种类型，而西方心理学的科学话语是描述性语言。将导引性语言当作科学命题来处理，或将科学描述等同于修行指引，都是根本性的误读。

**第三，自我观的根本对立**。西方心理学总体将某种形式的**自我感**视为心理健康的必要条件；唯识学则从根本上质疑了这一预设的合法性：末那识所执取的”自我”，在圆成实的层面是虚妄的，修行的终极目标是如实认识这一虚妄性，从而解除对它的执取。这不是”虚无主义”，而是三性分析下更为精密的存在论立场。³

> **EN对应 · S1.2 Three Foundational Methodological Commitments**
>
> Three methodological commitments must be stated before proceeding to comparative analysis, since they govern every subsequent discussion:
>
> **First, the priority of teleological difference.** Yogācāra’s ultimate aim is **liberation** (mokṣa) — complete release from the suffering of conditioned existence, manifested as āśraya-parāvṛtti (the transformation of the basis), the fundamental transformation of the ālayavijñāna. Western psychology’s goals — whether Freud’s “where id was, there ego shall be,” Maslow’s self-actualization, or Seligman’s flourishing — are oriented toward psychological health and functional improvement within this life, with no reference to liberation in the soteriological sense. This difference is foundational; any comparative work that ignores it will systematically misread both traditions.
>
> **Second, the asymmetry of language types.** Buddhist canonical language includes at least three functionally distinct types: analytical-definitional language (pursuing internal consistency and conceptual clarity), directive language (whose function is to induce specific experiential shifts in the practitioner, not to characterize objects), and provisional discourse (upāya, employed to counter specific attachments in specific audiences). Western psychological discourse is paradigmatically descriptive language, pursuing operational definitions reducible to repeatable measurement procedures. Forcing directive language into the frame of scientific propositions, or treating scientific descriptions as equivalent to contemplative guidance, generates systematic distortion in both directions.³
>
> **Third, the fundamental opposition of conceptions of self.** Western psychology, across its major traditions, treats some form of **self-cohesion** as a necessary condition of psychological health — Freud’s strong ego, Jung’s integrated Self, Rogers’ authentic self, Maslow’s actualized self. Yogācāra fundamentally challenges the legitimacy of this presupposition: the “self” grasped by manas-vijñāna is, at the level of pariniṣpanna-svabhāva (the perfected nature), fabricated (without self-nature); the ultimate aim of practice is to recognize this fabrication precisely and release attachment to it. This is not nihilism — it is a more refined analysis through the three natures — but it constitutes an irreducible philosophical opposition that cannot be dissolved by interpretive goodwill.
>

## 第二节：方法论框架——比较的条件与标准

> **主题对读注 · 三类对话框架在心理学的运用**
> 本文第二节将跨传统对话分为结构同构、相互启发、直接实证对应三类，并特别指出**心理学比较的特殊困难**：心理学的对象（心灵）与唯识学以第一人称实证为方法，二者在“观察者”身份上不对称；本节英文原句全文已由「EN对应」块逐小节给出。

### 一、共同问题域的优先性

两个传统的对话，建立在共同面对的**人类处境问题**之上：认知的系统性局限、苦难的普遍性、自我感的脆弱与建构性，以及在无常流变中寻求理解的根本驱动。这是对话得以可能的文明基础，也是防止对话沦为概念游戏的最终尺度。

然而，共同的问题域并不意味着共同的解决路径。西方心理学与唯识学对”有效回应”的理解存在根本分歧：前者通过外部程序积累可传递的知识；后者的核心是修行者自身的转化，窥基在《成唯识论述记》中一再区分”证”与”解”，这一区分在任何认真的比较研究中都不可忽视。⁴


> **EN对应 · S2.1 The Shared Human Problematic as the Ground of Dialogue**
>
> Dialogue between these traditions is grounded in the **shared human existential problematic** they each address: the structural limitations of cognition, the pervasiveness and unavoidability of suffering, the fragility and constructedness of the sense of self, and the fundamental drive to seek understanding within impermanence. This shared orientation provides the civilizational foundation for encounter, and also serves as the final measure against which the encounter’s value must be assessed.
>
> Yet shared problems do not entail shared solutions. Kuiji’s *Cheng Weishi Lun Shuji* repeatedly insists on the distinction between *jie* (conceptual understanding) and *zheng* (direct realization): no accumulation of theoretical mastery substitutes for the lived accomplishment of liberation. The Venerable Yinshun made clear in *The Origin of Yogācāra* that Yogācāra’s epistemological analysis never operates as a purely philosophical exercise but always serves the liberative goal of practice.⁴ Ouyang Jingwu famously argued that Buddhist teaching is “neither religion nor philosophy,” possessing both philosophical precision and soteriological concern in a combination neither category alone can exhaust.⁵ These reminders are essential methodological guardrails for any serious comparative enterprise.
### 二、三类对话关系的分析框架

依据《识与心灵》所建立的方法论框架，本文将两个传统间的所有比较区分为三种认识论类型：

**结构同构**（structural homology）：两个传统在形式关系上呈现出可观察的相似性，但不要求两者描述同一对象，也不要求可以相互推导。结构同构是提出新研究问题的启发性资源，而非论证的终点。将结构同构误读为相互证明，是比较研究中最常见的方法论错误。

**相互启发**（mutual illumination）：一个传统的问题框架使另一个传统能够在自身框架内提出原本无法提出的问题，或以更高精度处理原本模糊的议题。相互启发要求双向性，且产生可追踪的问题转化。

**直接实证对应**（direct empirical correspondence）：两者在同一描述层次上形成可验证的、原则上可证伪的对应。这是认识论要求最高、也最为罕见的类型。


> **EN对应 · S2.2 Three Types of Cross-Traditional Dialogue**
>
> Following the methodological framework developed in the companion paper *Vijñāna and Mind*,⁶ this paper distinguishes three analytically distinct types of cross-traditional comparison:
>
> **Structural homology**: a formal resemblance between conceptual structures in the two traditions that neither requires both to describe the same object nor licenses mutual entailment. Structural homology is a heuristic resource for generating research questions, not a terminus of argument. Treating homology as mutual confirmation — the “homology fallacy” — is the most prevalent methodological error in existing comparative literature.
>
> **Mutual illumination**: one tradition’s conceptual framework enables the other, working within its own framework, to formulate questions it could not previously ask, or to handle existing questions with greater precision. Mutual illumination requires traceable, bidirectional problem transformation.
>
> **Direct empirical correspondence**: descriptions in both traditions correspond at the same level of description in a way that is in principle falsifiable. By this standard, genuinely realized instances in existing comparative literature are exceedingly rare.
### 三、心理学比较的特殊困难

相较于《识与心灵》所处理的意识科学与AI哲学比较，唯识学与心理学的比较面临三个特殊困难：

**第一，心理学本身的内部多元性**。西方心理学不是一个统一的体系，而是包含多个范式、多个学派、多种方法论取向的复合传统。任何将”西方心理学”整体与唯识学比较的尝试，都必须精确指定是哪个心理学传统在进行对话。

**第二，临床有效性与哲学真理的混淆**。正念认知疗法（MBCT）对抑郁症复发的临床有效性，不能被解读为唯识学形而上学主张的科学验证；反之，唯识学对心理过程的精密分析，也不能被简单化约为临床心理学的理论基础。两者之间的关系，需要精确的认识论定位。

**第三，”佛教心理学”标签的混乱**。在当代心理学文献中，”Buddhist psychology”这一标签往往混合了南传阿毘达磨、唯识学、禅宗、藏传佛教等完全不同的传统，而不同传统对心理过程的分析存在重大差异。本文将尽可能精确区分唯识学（护法-玄奘系为主）与其他佛教传统。

---


> **EN对应 · S2.3 Three Special Difficulties in Psychological Comparison**
>
> Relative to comparisons with consciousness science or AI philosophy, the comparison of Yogācāra with psychology faces three distinctive methodological difficulties:
>
> **First, the internal plurality of Western psychology itself.** Western psychology is not a unified system but a complex of multiple paradigms, schools, and methodological orientations. Any comparative claim must specify precisely which psychological tradition is participating in the dialogue.
>
> **Second, the conflation of clinical efficacy with philosophical truth.** The clinical effectiveness of Mindfulness-Based Cognitive Therapy for depression prevention cannot be read as scientific validation of Yogācāra metaphysical claims; conversely, Yogācāra’s analytical refinement cannot be simply reframed as clinical theory. The epistemological relationship between the two requires careful positioning.
>
> **Third, the “Buddhist psychology” label problem.** In contemporary psychological literature, the label “Buddhist psychology” conflates Theravāda Abhidharma, Yogācāra, Chan, and Tibetan traditions whose analyses of psychological process diverge substantially. This paper maintains as precise a focus as possible on Yogācāra in the Dharmapāla-Xuanzang lineage, noting divergences from other traditions where relevant.
>

## 第三节：精神分析传统与唯识学——从弗洛伊德到关系精神分析

### 一、弗洛伊德精神分析：奠基与局限

弗洛伊德（Sigmund Freud，1856–1939）的精神分析，以无意识（the Unconscious）为核心概念，以本能冲动（drives/Triebe）为动力，以压抑（repression）为主要防御机制。⁵ 其地形学模型（1915年）将心灵划分为意识、前意识与无意识三个层次；结构模型（1923年）将心灵划分为本我（id）、自我（ego）与超我（superego）。⁶

**阿赖耶识与弗洛伊德无意识：结构同构与本质差异**

两者都描述了一种潜在的、不进入常规意识觉察的心理层面，持续影响意识层面的思维与行为——这是真实的结构同构。⁷

然而，差异是根本性的：

弗洛伊德的无意识**内容**主要是被压抑的欲望（特别是性欲）与创伤记忆——充满张力与冲突的”热”内容。阿赖耶识则是**中性的**（avyākṛta）——《成唯识论》明确指出，阿赖耶识无覆无记，非善非恶，⁸ 储藏一切种子（善种、不善种、无记种皆有），但作为储藏库本身，不带有弗洛伊德式的压抑张力。

**产生机制**上，弗洛伊德的无意识内容主要通过”压抑”产生——某些无法被自我接受的内容被主动排斥出意识之外；阿赖耶识的种子通过”熏习”（vāsanā）积累——每一个经验在阿赖耶识中留下印记，这是自然的因果过程，不涉及压抑的动力学。

**治疗目标**上，精神分析以”增强自我”为方向——使理性的自我能够更好地管理本我的冲动（”本我所在，自我当在”）；唯识学修行的目标是去除末那识的我执，通过对识之本性的如实认识实现转依——这不是自我的强化，而是对”自我”这一建构本身的根本超越。

William Waldron 在《佛教无意识》（2003）中对这一比较做了迄今最为系统的学术处理，指出两者之间存在有价值的结构同构，但在内容性质、产生机制与理论目的上存在根本差异。⁹

> **术语格义 · 末那识（manas-vijñāna）**
> **梵** manas-vijñāna（manas：意·思量；vijñāna：识）；kliṣṭa-manas（染污意）
> **藏** ཉོན་མོངས་ལྡན་པའི་ཡིད།（nyon mongs ldan pa'i yid，“有烦恼之意”）
> **英** manas-consciousness · seventh consciousness · afflicted mind
> **日** 末那識（まなしき）
> **注** 七识，恒审思量，执阿赖耶见分为“我”。本文以末那识我执对照并问题化西方心理学“自我”预设。出处 CBETA T31n1585。
> **术语格义 · 我执四烦恼（ātmagrāha-catuṣka）**
> **梵** ātmagrāha（我执）· ātmasneha（我爱）· ātmamāna（我慢）· ātmamoha（我痴）
> **英** self-grasping · self-love · self-conceit · self-delusion
> **日** 我執·我愛·我慢·我痴
> **注** 末那识所具四根本烦恼，恒与末那相应，执阿赖耶见分为实我。出处 CBETA T31n1585 卷四。

**末那识与自我：最深刻的哲学对立**

末那识（manas-vijñāna，第七识）持续地、不间断地执取阿赖耶识的见分为恒常不变的”自我”，构成我执（ātmagrāha）、我爱（ātmasneha）、我慢（ātmamāna）与我痴（ātmamoha）四根本烦恼的来源。¹⁰

与弗洛伊德的自我（ego）相比：弗洛伊德的自我是现实原则的执行者，是理性与适应的代表，是心理健康的**目标**；末那识则恰恰相反——它是虚妄执取的**来源**，是修行需要对治的核心对象。

这一根本差异，揭示了两个传统在”心理健康标准”上的最深分歧：西方精神分析以自我的健康功能为目标，唯识学以我执的根本消解为目标。前者是在既有自我结构内寻求优化，后者是对这一结构本身的存在合法性提出根本质疑。

**弗洛伊德的”海洋感”与佛教修行体验**

弗洛伊德晚年与罗曼·罗兰（Romain Rolland）的通信中讨论的”海洋感”（oceanic feeling）¹¹，提供了一个精神分析与佛教对话的有趣入口。弗洛伊德将这种无边界、与宇宙融合的体验解释为婴儿期自我-世界无分化状态的退行；佛教传统则将类似的体验理解为对本性的直接认识，是修行成就的标志。这两种解读折射出两个传统在”自我解构”是退行还是成就这一根本问题上的深层对立，也预示了整个比较研究的核心张力。


> **EN对应 · S3.1 Freudian Psychoanalysis: Foundation and Limits**
>
> Sigmund Freud’s (1856–1939) psychoanalysis centers on the Unconscious, instinctual drives (Triebe), and repression as the primary defensive mechanism.⁷ His topographic model (1915) divides the mind into conscious, preconscious, and unconscious layers; his structural model (1923) articulates the tripartite id, ego, and superego.⁸
>
> **Ālayavijñāna and the Freudian Unconscious: Structural Homology and Essential Difference**
>
> Both traditions describe a latent psychological stratum, inaccessible to ordinary conscious awareness, that persistently influences conscious thought and behavior — a genuine structural homology.⁹ Yet the differences are more fundamental than the resemblances:
>
> The **content** of Freud’s Unconscious consists primarily of repressed desires (especially sexual) and traumatic memories — “hot” material charged with tension and conflict. The ālayavijñāna is **ethically neutral** (avyākṛta): the *Cheng Weishi Lun* explicitly states that the ālayavijñāna is unobscured and indeterminate (無覆無記), neither wholesome nor unwholesome.¹⁰ It stores seeds of all types (wholesome, unwholesome, and indeterminate), but as a repository it carries none of Freud’s repressive dynamics.
>
> The **generative mechanism** also differs fundamentally. Freudian unconscious content is primarily produced through *repression* — the active exclusion of unacceptable material from consciousness. Ālayavijñāna seeds accumulate through *perfuming* (vāsanā) — a natural causal process in which each experience leaves a latent impression in the store-consciousness, without any repressive dynamics.
>
> Most consequentially, the **therapeutic aims** are opposed. Psychoanalysis aims at ego-strengthening — enabling the rational ego to better manage id impulses (”Where id was, there ego shall be”). Yogācāra practice aims at eliminating the self-grasping of manas — through precise recognition of the nature of vijñāna, achieving āśraya-parāvṛtti. This is not the strengthening of self but the fundamental dissolution of the self-construction.
>
> William Waldron’s *The Buddhist Unconscious* (2003) provides the most systematic scholarly treatment of this comparison, concluding that genuine structural homology exists but that content, mechanism, and therapeutic purpose differ fundamentally.¹¹
>
> **Manas and the Ego: The Most Profound Philosophical Opposition**
>
> Manas-vijñāna (the seventh consciousness) continuously and without interruption grasps the subjective aspect (darśana-bhāga) of the ālayavijñāna as a permanent, unchanging “self,” constituting the structural root of ātmagrāha (self-grasping), ātmasneha (self-love), ātmamāna (self-conceit), and ātmamoha (self-delusion).¹²
>
> Compared with Freud’s ego: the ego is the executor of the reality principle, representative of rationality and adaptation, the **goal** of psychological health. Manas is the precise opposite — the **source** of fictitious grasping, the primary object that practice must counter. This fundamental difference reveals the deepest divergence between the two traditions on what “psychological health” means: psychoanalysis aims at healthy ego functioning; Yogācāra aims at the fundamental dissolution of self-grasping.
>
> **Freud’s “Oceanic Feeling” and Buddhist Contemplative Experience**
>
> Freud’s late discussion, in correspondence with Romain Rolland, of the “oceanic feeling” — the experience of boundlessness and merger with the universe¹³ — provides an illuminating entry point. Freud interpreted this experience as regression to the infant’s undifferentiated ego-world state; Buddhist tradition understands analogous experiences as direct recognition of the nature of mind, the fruit of contemplative accomplishment. This interpretive opposition — whether self-dissolution represents regression or achievement — prefigures the central tension that runs through the entire comparative enterprise.
### 二、客体关系理论：从驱力到关系

克莱因（Melanie Klein，1882–1960）、温尼科特（Donald Winnicott，1896–1971）与比昂（Wilfred Bion，1897–1979）等人发展的**客体关系理论**（Object Relations Theory），将心理发展的焦点从弗洛伊德的本能驱力，转向**早期关系经验**对内在世界的建构。¹²

温尼科特的”足够好的母亲”（good-enough mother）概念，强调照顾者的镜映功能（mirroring function）对婴儿自我感形成的关键作用；比昂的”容纳”（containment）理论，描述了母亲如何接收并转化婴儿无法承受的心理内容，使之成为可消化的经验。¹³

这与唯识学的几个概念形成了有价值的结构对照：

温尼科特的”过渡性客体”（transitional object）——介于”我”与”非我”之间的中间经验地带——与唯识学的依他起性概念有深刻的共鸣：两者都描述了一种不完全主观也不完全客观的经验现实，都拒绝了简单的主客二分。

比昂的”阿尔法功能”（alpha function）——将原始心理内容（beta elements）转化为可思考的元素（alpha elements）的心理容纳能力——在功能上类似于唯识学中意识（第六识）对前五识输入的整合与分别，但唯识学对这一过程有更精密的机制分析（四分说、三性分析），也有更系统的修行路径（如何从遍计所执走向依他起的如实认识）。


> **EN对应 · S3.2 Object Relations Theory: From Drive to Relationship**
>
> Klein’s (1882–1960), Winnicott’s (1896–1971), and Bion’s (1897–1979) object relations theories shifted psychoanalytic attention from instinctual drives to the formative influence of early relational experience on the internal world.¹⁴
>
> Winnicott’s concept of the “good-enough mother” and its mirroring function resonates with Yogācāra’s account of how the first-person sense of self is constituted through the reflexive activity of manas — both accounts locate the genesis of self-experience in a relational dynamic rather than in an isolated psychic container. Bion’s “alpha function” — the capacity to receive and metabolize primitive psychic contents (beta elements) into thinkable alpha elements — parallels, in certain functional respects, the integrative and discriminative activity of the sixth consciousness (manas-vijñāna’s synthetic function), though Yogācāra provides a far more systematic analysis of the underlying mechanism (the four-aspect analysis, the three natures) and a systematic practice path for transforming it.
>
> Winnicott’s “transitional object” — the intermediate zone of experience between “me” and “not-me”¹⁵ — resonates with Yogācāra’s paratantra-svabhāva: both describe an experiential reality that is neither fully subjective nor fully objective, both refuse the simple subject-object dichotomy. This constitutes a genuine case of mutual illumination, with implications for how the “neither real nor unreal” character of dependent arising can be communicated within psychological discourse.
### 三、科胡特自体心理学：自恋与自体

海因茨·科胡特（Heinz Kohut，1913–1981）创立的**自体心理学**（Self Psychology），将关注焦点从驱力冲突转向**自体凝聚性**（self-cohesion）的发展与维持。¹⁴ 科胡特认为，健康的心理发展需要足够的镜映（mirroring）、理想化（idealization）与孪生（twinship）体验，自恋（narcissism）在其框架中不只是病理，也是发展的必要阶段。

科胡特的”悲剧人”（tragic man）概念——一个在追求自体实现的过程中遭遇命运局限的人，而非弗洛伊德”罪恶人”（guilty man）那样陷入冲突的人¹⁵——与唯识学的苦难观有有趣的对照：唯识学将苦（duḥkha）根植于末那识我执的虚妄性，而非道德意义上的”罪恶”；科胡特将心理苦恼根植于自体需要的受挫，而非本能的压抑。两者都超越了简单的道德框架来理解人类苦恼，但对”苦恼的根源”的分析方向根本不同——科胡特认为自体需要是真实的、应当被满足的；唯识学认为末那识对独立自体的执取本身是虚妄的。


> **EN对应 · S3.3 Kohut’s Self Psychology: Narcissism and the Cohesive Self**
>
> Heinz Kohut’s (1913–1981) self psychology shifted analytic attention to **self-cohesion**: healthy development requires sufficient mirroring, idealizing, and twinship experiences, with narcissism reconceived not as pathology alone but as a developmental necessity.¹⁶
>
> Kohut’s “tragic man” — a being encountering the limits of fate in the pursuit of self-realization, as opposed to Freud’s “guilty man” caught in conflict¹⁷ — enters a productive dialogue with Yogācāra’s account of suffering: Yogācāra locates duḥkha in the fabricated nature of the self grasped by manas, not in moral transgression; Kohut locates psychological suffering in the frustration of genuine self-needs, not in instinctual conflict. Both transcend moralistic frameworks for understanding mental anguish — but their analyses of its source point in opposite directions. Kohut treats self-needs as real and legitimate, warranting satisfaction; Yogācāra holds that manas’ grasping at an independent self is precisely the fabrication that generates suffering and requires dissolution.
### 四、关系精神分析与间主观性

斯蒂芬·米切尔（Stephen Mitchell，1946–2000）、杰西卡·本杰明（Jessica Benjamin）等人发展的**关系精神分析**（Relational Psychoanalysis），强调心灵本质上是关系性的、间主观的，而非孤立的容器。¹⁶ 本杰明的”相互承认”（mutual recognition）理论，论证了真实的相遇需要承认他者作为独立主体的他性（otherness）。¹⁷

这与唯识学的共相种子理论形成了深刻的对话空间：关系精神分析描述的”相互构成的主体性”（mutually constituted subjectivity），与唯识学的”众生共业共同变现器世间”有结构上的共鸣——两者都拒绝孤立的、自给自足的主体概念，都强调心理现实的关系性建构。

然而，差异同样重要：关系精神分析的关系性，仍然是在主体与主体之间的关系框架内运作，预设了真实存在的主体相互影响；唯识学的共相种子理论，则在更根本的层次上质疑了独立主体本身的自性——不是主体在关系中被构成，而是”主体”这一概念本身是识变的产物，没有独立于识的构成性活动的自在存在。


> **EN对应 · S3.4 Relational Psychoanalysis and Intersubjectivity**
>
> Stephen Mitchell’s (1946–2000) and Jessica Benjamin’s relational psychoanalysis emphasizes the inherently relational, intersubjective character of mind.¹⁸ Benjamin’s theory of “mutual recognition” — that genuine encounter requires acknowledging the other as an independent subject with genuine otherness¹⁹ — resonates with Yogācāra’s shared seeds theory, which likewise refuses any account of mind as an isolated, self-sufficient container.
>
> Yet the philosophical depth of Yogācāra’s account exceeds relational psychoanalysis at a crucial point. Relational psychoanalysis operates within a framework of subjects mutually influencing each other — it presupposes genuinely existing subjects that stand in relation. Yogācāra’s shared seeds theory (sādhāraṇa-bīja) challenges the independent selfhood of the relating subjects themselves: it is not that subjects constitute each other through relation, but that “subjecthood” is itself a product of vijñāna-transformation, without any self-nature independent of the constitutive activity of vijñāna. This is a more radical intersubjectivity than relational psychoanalysis envisages — what might be called **intersubjectivity without ontological subjects**.
### 五、神经精神分析：连接弗洛伊德与神经科学

马克·索尔姆斯（Mark Solms）发展的**神经精神分析**（Neuropsychoanalysis），尝试将弗洛伊德的精神分析理论与当代神经科学相结合，特别是与潘克塞普的情感神经科学进行整合。¹⁸ 索尔姆斯发现，弗洛伊德关于本我（id）的描述，与大脑中缘下皮层（subcortical limbic circuits）的功能高度对应；梦的功能在于维持睡眠状态下的心理平衡，而非（如弗洛伊德认为的）实现愿望。

这一方向为唯识学与神经科学的对话提供了新的中介：若弗洛伊德的无意识与阿赖耶识之间存在结构同构，若弗洛伊德无意识的神经基础是皮层下情感回路，则我们可以提出一个更精确的神经-唯识学对应假说：**阿赖耶识的熏习机制，在神经层面可能对应于皮层下情感回路（Panksepp的SEEKING系统、FEAR系统等）通过杏仁核、海马体与前额叶的连接，对皮层功能产生持久影响的神经可塑性过程**。这是一个可以被进一步研究的具体假说，属于”相互启发”类型的对话。

---


> **EN对应 · S3.5 Neuropsychoanalysis: Bridging Freud and Neuroscience**
>
> Mark Solms’ neuropsychoanalysis attempts to integrate Freudian theory with contemporary neuroscience, particularly Panksepp’s affective neuroscience.²⁰ Solms finds that Freud’s characterization of the id corresponds closely to the functions of subcortical limbic circuits; dream function serves to maintain psychological equilibrium during sleep rather than (as Freud thought) to fulfill wishes.
>
> This provides a new mediating bridge for Yogācāra-neuroscience dialogue: if Freud’s unconscious and the ālayavijñāna share structural homology, and if the neural basis of the Freudian unconscious involves subcortical affective circuits, then a more precise neurological-Yogācāra correspondence hypothesis becomes available: **the ālayavijñāna’s perfuming mechanism may correspond, at the neural level, to the process by which subcortical affective circuits (Panksepp’s SEEKING, FEAR, and RAGE systems) produce lasting modifications of cortical function through amygdala-hippocampus-prefrontal connectivity — the neural expression of karmic plasticity**. This is a specific, testable hypothesis belonging to the “mutual illumination” category of dialogue.
>

## 第四节：现象学与存在主义心理学——第一人称视角的西方传统

### 一、现象学心理学：胡塞尔的遗产

胡塞尔（Edmund Husserl，1859–1938）的现象学，以对意识经验结构的精密描述为核心方法，提出了**意向性**（intentionality）概念——意识总是”关于某物的意识”——作为意识活动的基本特征。¹⁹ 这与唯识学的”相分”（客观面）与”见分”（主观面）的四分说，在对意识活动结构的分析上，形成了可以精确对应的比较基础：唯识学的”见分缘相分”，正是对意向性结构的另一种表达——但唯识学更进一步，以”自证分”（证自证分）分析了意向性活动的自我认知结构，这是胡塞尔的意向性分析所不曾达到的精度。

Lusthaus 在《佛教现象学》（2002）中，对《成唯识论》与胡塞尔现象学的系统比较，至今仍是这一领域最重要的学术参考。²⁰ 他的核心结论是：两个传统在方法论上（都是对意识活动结构的精密反思性分析）最为接近，但在最终目标上存在根本差异——胡塞尔保留了一个构成性的”纯粹自我”（pure ego），唯识学从根本上否认了任何独立自存的自我（无我）。

Zahavi（2005）对这一比较有进一步的批判性分析，认为”最小自我”（minimal self）概念——意识流中不可消除的第一人称视角——在现象学与唯识学之间构成了最深也最难通约的分歧点。²¹

**发生现象学与种子熏习：一个被忽视的平行**

胡塞尔晚期的”发生现象学”（genetic phenomenology）研究意识经验如何通过”沉淀”（sedimentation）形成习惯性结构（Habitualität），这些习惯性结构反过来影响未来的经验构成方式。²² 这与唯识学的”种子生现行，现行熏种子”的循环机制，在形式结构上存在深刻的平行性——两者都描述了经验如何在意识结构中留下持久的印记，并通过这种印记影响未来的经验方式。差异在于：胡塞尔的沉淀是在”纯粹自我”的构成性活动框架内发生的；唯识学的熏习是无我的因果过程，没有任何构成性主体作为其承担者。


> **EN对应 · S4.1 Phenomenological Psychology: Husserl’s Legacy**
>
> Husserl’s (1859–1938) phenomenology, centered on the disciplined description of intentional consciousness, advances **intentionality** as the defining feature of conscious acts — consciousness is always consciousness *of* something.²¹ This corresponds, within Yogācāra’s four-aspect analysis, to the relation between the objective aspect (xiangfen, 相分) and the subjective aspect (jianfen, 見分): the “subjective aspect apprehending the objective aspect” is a precise structural expression of intentionality. But Yogācāra goes further, articulating a self-witnessing aspect (sva-saṃvedana, 自證分) and a self-witnessing of the self-witnessing (證自證分), providing an analysis of the self-cognizing structure of intentional activity that Husserl’s intentionality analysis never achieves.
>
> Lusthaus’ *Buddhist Phenomenology* (2002) remains the indispensable scholarly reference for this comparison.²² His core conclusion — that the two traditions are methodologically closest (both are rigorous reflexive analyses of the structure of conscious activity) while diverging most fundamentally at the level of ultimate aim (Husserl retains a constituting “pure ego”; Yogācāra denies any independently existing self) — frames the terms of dialogue with exemplary precision.
>
> **Genetic Phenomenology and Seed Perfuming: A Neglected Parallel**
>
> Husserl’s late “genetic phenomenology” investigates how conscious experience, through *sedimentation* (Ablagerung), forms habitual structures (Habitualitäten) that retroactively influence the constitution of future experience.²³ This exhibits deep formal parallelism with Yogācāra’s “seeds generating manifestations, manifestations perfuming seeds” (種子生現行，現行熏種子) cycle — both describe how experience leaves persistent impressions in the structure of consciousness that shape subsequent experience. The critical difference: Husserl’s sedimentation occurs within the constituting activity of a “pure ego”; Yogācāra’s perfuming is a causally conditioned process without self-nature and without any subjectival bearer.
### 二、存在主义心理学：死亡、自由与意义

**欧文·亚隆（Irvin Yalom）的存在主义治疗**

亚隆（1931– ）在《存在主义心理治疗》（*Existential Psychotherapy*, 1980）中，识别了四个”存在性终极关怀”（ultimate concerns）：**死亡**（death）、**自由**（freedom）、**孤独**（isolation）与**无意义**（meaninglessness）。²³ 他认为，这四个存在性困境是人类心理苦恼的深层来源，任何真正有深度的心理治疗都必须正视这些终极关怀。

这与唯识学的苦恼根源分析形成了有趣的对话：

**关于死亡**：亚隆认为死亡焦虑是人类心理防御机制的根本驱动力；唯识学对死亡的处理更为精密——死亡（命终）是阿赖耶识离开有根身的过程，而阿赖耶识本身作为识流并不”消亡”，轮回的继续正是因为我执与业力种子的持续。唯识修行对死亡的态度，不是通过”克服死亡焦虑”而是通过”如实认识识流的本性”来超越生死的执取。

**关于自由**：亚隆强调人类面对自身选择的”存在性自由”（existential freedom），这种自由令人焦虑；唯识学对”自由”有完全不同的理解框架——在唯识学中，凡夫（pṛthagjana）受业力种子的推动，末那识的我执使一切”自由选择”都在自我中心的框架内运作，真正的自由（解脱）是通过转依实现的，是从业力束缚中的解放，而非存在主义意义上的个体选择自由。

**关于孤独**：亚隆区分了人际孤独（interpersonal isolation）、内心孤独（intrapersonal isolation）与存在性孤独（existential isolation，每个存在者最终必须独自面对自身的存在）；唯识学的共相种子理论提供了一个有趣的反论——即便在存在论层面，识流也不是孤立的，共业的熏习使每个识流与所有其他识流深刻地相互关联，”存在性孤独”在唯识学的框架中是遍计所执性层面的虚妄执取，而非依他起层面的真实处境。

**维克托·弗兰克尔的意义治疗**

弗兰克尔（Viktor Frankl，1905–1997）的意义疗法（Logotherapy），以寻找**意义**（meaning）作为人类最根本的动机，认为即便在最极端的苦难中，人也可以通过选择态度的方式找到意义。²⁴

这与唯识学的苦难观有值得深思的对照：唯识学不鼓励人在苦难中寻找意义（这仍然是在苦难的框架内运作），而是通过识别苦难的根源（我执）来根本超越苦难框架；弗兰克尔的意义治疗假设了一个能够选择的自我，而唯识学质疑这个”选择的自我”本身的实在性。然而，两者都拒绝了纯粹的唯快乐主义，都指向某种超越当下苦乐的更深层价值——在这一点上，有真实的共鸣。


> **EN对应 · S4.2 Existential Psychology: Death, Freedom, and Meaning**
>
> **Irvin Yalom’s Existential Therapy**
>
> Yalom’s (1931–) *Existential Psychotherapy* (1980) identifies four “ultimate concerns”: **death**, **freedom**, **isolation**, and **meaninglessness**.²⁴ He argues that these existential givens constitute the deep sources of psychological suffering, and that any therapy of genuine depth must confront them.
>
> The dialogue with Yogācāra is revealing at each point:
>
> On **death**: Yalom treats death anxiety as the primary engine of psychological defense; Yogācāra’s treatment is more precise — death (命終) is the separation of the ālayavijñāna from the sentient body with its faculties, while the vijñāna-stream itself continues, driven by karmic seeds and the self-grasping of manas. Yogācāra practice does not “overcome death anxiety” but dissolves the self-grasping that makes death threatening.
>
> On **freedom**: Yalom’s “existential freedom” — the anxiety-inducing responsibility of being the author of one’s choices — is reframed in Yogācāra as the recognition that ordinary “choices” are conditioned expressions of karmic seeds and manas’ self-grasping, and that genuine freedom (liberation) is achieved through āśraya-parāvṛtti, not through the more vigorous exercise of ego-agency.
>
> On **isolation**: Yalom’s “existential isolation” — the ultimate aloneness of each being before its own existence — is countered by the Yogācāra shared-seeds theory, which shows that vijñāna-streams are, at their deepest level, co-constitutive through shared karma. The experience of radical existential isolation is, in Yogācāra’s analysis, a product of the imagined nature’s reification of a separate self — a parikalpita-level fabrication rather than a paratantra-level reality.
>
> **Viktor Frankl’s Logotherapy**
>
> Frankl’s (1905–1997) Logotherapy holds meaning-seeking as the fundamental human motivation, maintaining that even under conditions of extreme suffering, human beings can find meaning through the attitude they choose toward their circumstances.²⁵
>
> The comparison with Yogācāra is instructive: Yogācāra does not encourage finding meaning within suffering (this operates within the framework of suffering), but aims at recognizing the source of suffering (me-grasping) so as to fundamentally transcend the suffering-framework. Frankl presupposes a self capable of choosing, while Yogācāra questions the reality of this “choosing self.” Yet both reject pure hedonism and point toward some dimension of value transcending immediate pleasure and pain — a genuine point of convergence, though the philosophical paths diverge sharply.
### 三、格式塔心理学：当下完形与唯识的了别

弗里兹·珀尔斯（Fritz Perls，1893–1970）的格式塔治疗（Gestalt Therapy），强调**当下觉察**（present-awareness）、**接触**（contact）与**完形的形成与完成**（formation and completion of gestalt）。²⁵ 格式塔治疗的核心技术——关注此时此地的直接体验，不被过去的未竟事宜或对未来的担忧所扰——与佛教的正念修行有表面上的相似性，也确实相互影响了当代冥想心理学的发展。

从唯识学的角度分析，格式塔的”当下觉察”对应于第六识的了别活动在当下时刻的专注；然而，格式塔对”完形”的追求——确保每一个未完成的体验得到完成——在唯识学看来，仍然是在遍计所执的框架内运作，把经验内容执为需要被”完成”的实体。唯识的观（vipaśyanā），恰恰是不再追求”完成”任何”完形”，而是如实观察经验的缘起性与无常性。

---


> **EN对应 · S4.3 Gestalt Therapy: Present Awareness and Vijñāna’s Discrimination**
>
> Fritz Perls’ (1893–1970) Gestalt Therapy emphasizes **present-moment awareness** (present-awareness), **contact**, and the **formation and completion of gestalt**.²⁶ Gestalt therapy’s core technique — attending to direct, present experience without distraction by past unfinished business or future anticipation — bears surface resemblance to Buddhist mindfulness practice and has indeed influenced contemporary contemplative psychology.
>
> From a Yogācāra perspective, Gestalt’s “present awareness” corresponds to the sixth consciousness’s discriminative activity focused in the present moment. However, Gestalt’s pursuit of “completing the gestalt” — ensuring that each incomplete experience reaches closure — operates, in Yogācāra’s analysis, within the framework of the imagined nature: it treats experiential contents as entities requiring “completion.” Yogācāra’s vipaśyanā (insight meditation) is precisely the practice of no longer pursuing the “completion” of any “gestalt,” but observing with precision the dependent-arising and impermanent character of all experience.
>

## 第五节：行为主义与认知心理学——从刺激-反应到预测处理

### 一、行为主义：方法论与唯识学的意外共鸣

行为主义（Behaviorism）以华生（1913年）的宣言为起点，以斯金纳的操作性条件作用（operant conditioning）为核心机制，将心理学限定为对可观察行为的研究。²⁶

行为主义与唯识学的直接对话空间有限，但有两点值得关注：

其一，斯金纳的**强化程序**与唯识学的**习气种子**（vāsanā）机制，在”过去经验塑造当前倾向”的结构上有表面相似性——但行为主义描述的是可观察的行为倾向，唯识学描述的是识流中有感受性色调的潜在印记，两者处于根本不同的描述层次。

其二，行为主义对”内在自我”的方法论拒绝（为了科学的可操作性），与唯识学对末那识我执的存在论批判（因为”自我”在认识论上根本不存在），在否定某种固定自我实体方面有意外的共鸣——尽管两者的出发点完全不同。


> **EN对应 · S5.1 Behaviorism: Methodological Parallels**
>
> Behaviorism, inaugurated by Watson’s 1913 manifesto and developed through Skinner’s operant conditioning, restricts psychology to the study of observable behavior.²⁷
>
> Direct dialogue space between behaviorism and Yogācāra is limited, but two points deserve attention. Skinner’s **reinforcement schedules** and Yogācāra’s **vāsanā** (habitual impressions) mechanism share the formal structure of “past experience shaping present dispositions” — but behaviorism describes observable behavioral tendencies, while Yogācāra describes affectively colored latent impressions within the vijñāna-stream. The levels of description are fundamentally different.
>
> More interestingly, behaviorism’s methodological rejection of the “inner self” (for the sake of scientific operationalizability) and Yogācāra’s ontological critique of the manas-constructed self (because self-nature is absent) unexpectedly converge in denying certain fixed self-entities — though from entirely different starting points.
### 二、认知心理学与认知疗法：三性说的精密对应

认知心理学以信息处理模型为理论框架，以贝克的认知疗法为临床代表，关注**认知图式**（cognitive schemas）、**自动思维**（automatic thoughts）与**认知歪曲**（cognitive distortions）对情绪与行为的影响。²⁷

**认知图式与遍计所执性**

贝克的认知图式——影响个体对经验进行编码与解释的深层认知结构——与唯识学的**遍计所执性**（parikalpita-svabhāva）有最深刻的结构共鸣。²⁸ 两者都描述了一种习惯性的、往往不被觉察的认知模式，这种模式使人以扭曲的方式看待自己、他人与世界，并由此产生情绪苦恼。

然而，差异同样重要：认知心理学的图式是可以通过认知重构加以修改的心理内容；唯识学的遍计所执，建立在末那识我执的基础上，其对治需要对我执本身的根本消解（无我的体证），而非仅仅是认知内容的替换。用认知重构来对治唯识意义上的遍计所执，相当于只是用一个新的遍计所执替换旧的遍计所执，而没有触及根本。

**认知歪曲与烦恼心所的精密对应**

贝克识别的认知歪曲与唯识学中烦恼心所（kleśa-caitta）的分类，形成了迄今比较研究中最精密的一组对应关系：

这一对应提示了两个传统在心理机制分析上存在可以相互启发的平行结构，但差异在于：认知疗法以改变认知内容为目标，唯识学以改变对认知内容的执取关系为目标——这是方向上的根本不同。

> **EN对应 · S5.2 Cognitive Psychology and Cognitive Therapy: The Precise Correspondence of the Three Natures**
>
> Cognitive psychology, with Beck’s cognitive therapy (1960s) and Ellis’s Rational Emotive Behavior Therapy (1955) as its clinical representatives, attends to **cognitive schemas**, **automatic thoughts**, and **cognitive distortions**.²⁸
>
> **Cognitive schemas and parikalpita-svabhāva**
>
> Beck’s cognitive schema — the deep cognitive structure influencing how an individual encodes and interprets experience — resonates most directly with Yogācāra’s **parikalpita-svabhāva** (imagined nature).²⁹ Both describe a habitual, largely unnoticed cognitive pattern that causes people to see themselves, others, and the world in distorted ways, generating emotional suffering.
>
> The critical difference: cognitive therapy treats schemas as modifiable mental contents addressable through cognitive restructuring; Yogācāra’s parikalpita-svabhāva is rooted in the structural self-grasping of manas, and its remedy requires the fundamental dissolution of me-grasping itself (the realization of anātman), not merely the replacement of one cognitive content by another. Using cognitive restructuring to address Yogācāra-level parikalpita amounts to substituting one form of imagined-nature fabrication for another, without touching the root.
>
> **Cognitive distortions and afflictive mental factors: a precision mapping**
>
> Beck’s catalogue of cognitive distortions and Yogācāra’s taxonomy of afflictive mental factors (kleśa-caitta) yield the most formally precise correspondence in existing comparative psychological literature:
>
> Beck’s Cognitive Distortion Yogācāra Correspondence Level Difference All-or-nothing thinking Parikalpita: absolute reification of both extremes Yogācāra focuses on the grasping act, not the content Overgeneralization Lobha (greed) and māna (conceit): expansive appropriative tendency Yogācāra provides more refined causal analysis Emotional reasoning Moha (delusion): the obscuring function Yogācāra analyzes to the level of fundamental ignorance (avidyā) Should statements Māna: normative-comparative grasping Yogācāra traces to the root of self-comparison Mind reading Vicikitsā (doubt/uncertainty): the unsteady function Similar in character, different in mechanism Labeling Saṃjñā (perception): solidifying conceptualization Yogācāra focuses on the fabricating tendency of saṃjñā Catastrophizing Dveṣa (aversion) and lobha (greed): affective polarization Yogācāra provides systematic causal tracing
>
> This mapping is suggestive rather than definitively precise, but it points to genuine structural parallelisms that can ground productive mutual illumination.
### 三、预测处理理论：最具创新性的比较前沿

> **术语格义 · 种子（bīja）与熏习（vāsanā）**
> **梵** bīja（种子）· vāsanā（熏习/习气）
> **藏** ས་བོན།（sa bon）· བག་ཆགས།（bag chags，习气）
> **英** seed · perfuming · habit-energy
> **日** 種子（しゅじ）· 習気（じっけ）
> **法** semence ¤〔待核〕
> **注** 本文第十一节提出预测处理理论（predictive processing / free-energy principle）与阿赖耶识种子机制的**结构同构**假说：预测模型之先验期待 ≈ 种子的成熟功能，预测误差更新 ≈ 新熏。此为原创性比较（T1），未经实证确认处标〔待核〕。

卡尔·弗里斯顿（Karl Friston）发展的**预测处理**（predictive processing）与**自由能原理**（free energy principle），是近年来认知神经科学中最具雄心的统一理论，试图以贝叶斯推断为基础，统一解释感知、行动、注意、学习乃至意识。²⁹ 其核心主张是：大脑不是被动地接受感官输入，而是持续地生成关于世界的**预测**（predictions），并通过最小化预测误差（prediction error）来更新内部模型。

这与唯识学的识变论（vijñāna-pariṇāma）形成了迄今最具哲学深度的结构同构：

**唯识的”识变”与预测处理的”预测生成”**，都描述了意识不是被动接受外部世界信息，而是主动建构经验的过程——识变论说”识从内部变现似境”，预测处理说”大脑从内部生成预测信号”。两者都颠覆了朴素实在论的”外部世界直接呈现于意识”的预设。

**阿赖耶识的种子与预测处理的”生成模型”（generative model）**：生成模型是大脑储存的关于世界的统计模式，用于生成预测；阿赖耶识的种子是过去经验留下的潜在印记，用于生起当下的现行（认知、情绪、行为）。两者都是”过去经验如何影响当下认知生成”的不同语言描述。³⁰

**精密性加权（precision weighting）与注意（manasikāra）**：预测处理理论中，注意被理解为对不同预测误差信号的精密性加权——决定哪些预测误差值得被处理；唯识学的作意（manasikāra）心所，是将心导向特定对象的心理功能，是五遍行心所之一，遍于一切心识活动。两者对”注意”的理解都指向一个选择性放大某些信息的过程，但机制分析处于不同层次。

**最重要的差异**：预测处理理论是一个第三人称的计算模型，描述信息如何在神经系统中被处理；唯识学是兼具第三人称分析与第一人称实践导向的认识论体系，关注识如何构成经验，以及如何通过修行转化这一构成过程。

> **梵** śamatha（止·寂止）· vipaśyanā（观·内观）
> **藏** ཞི་གནས།（zhi gnas，止）· ལྷག་མཐོང་།（lhag mthong，观）
> **英** calming / tranquility meditative stabilization · insight meditation

> **EN对应 · S5.3 Predictive Processing Theory: The Most Philosophically Deep Frontier**
>
> Karl Friston’s **predictive processing** and **free energy principle** represent perhaps the most ambitious unification theory in contemporary cognitive neuroscience, attempting to explain perception, action, attention, learning, and consciousness under a single Bayesian inference framework: the brain continuously generates **predictions** about the world and updates them by minimizing prediction error.³⁰
>
> This forms what is arguably the most philosophically deep structural isomorphism with Yogācāra yet identified in the comparative literature. The parallels are several and precise:
>
> **Vijñāna-transformation (vijñāna-pariṇāma) and predictive generation**: both doctrines hold that consciousness does not passively receive external world information but actively constructs experience from within. The vijñāna-transformation doctrine says “vijñāna manifests appearances from within”; predictive processing says “the brain generates predictive signals from within.” Both overturn naive realism’s assumption that the external world is directly presented to consciousness.
>
> **Ālayavijñāna seeds and the generative model**: the generative model is the brain’s stored statistical patterns about the world, used to generate predictions; ālayavijñāna seeds are latent impressions of past experience, used to generate current manifestations of cognition, emotion, and behavior. Both are different-language descriptions of “how past experience influences the generation of present cognition.” ³¹
>
> **Precision weighting and manasikāra (attention)**: in predictive processing, attention is understood as the precision weighting of different prediction error signals — determining which errors deserve processing; Yogācāra’s manasikāra (attention, one of the five universally present mental factors) is the psychological function that directs the mind toward specific objects. Both treat attention as a selective amplification process, though operating at different descriptive levels.
>
> **The most important difference**: predictive processing is a third-person computational model describing how information is processed in the nervous system; Yogācāra is an epistemological system with both third-person analytical and first-person practical orientations, addressing how vijñāna constitutes experience and how contemplative practice can transform this constitutive process.
>
> Yet predictive processing and Yogācāra may open a genuinely new research direction. If brain function can be understood as free energy minimization, and if the ālayavijñāna’s seed-manifestation-perfuming circuit can be understood as the brain’s generative model learning-update mechanism, then Yogācāra contemplative practice — particularly śamatha and vipaśyanā — can be reunderstood as **a systematic training process for updating the generative model**. Through stable concentration (śamatha), the system reduces the precision of sensory prediction errors; through insight observation (vipaśyanā), it updates the core generative model regarding “self” and “world.” This is not merely analogy but a framework capable of generating operationalizable research hypotheses — a genuine candidate for the “mutual illumination” category, potentially approaching “direct empirical correspondence” in certain respects.
>
> A specific testable prediction: compared with ordinary adults, practitioners who have realized advanced degrees of āśraya-parāvṛtti (long-term meditators) should show more flexible, lower-precision prior beliefs in their self-referential networks — weaker predictions of “stable self-existence,” wider acceptance of varied experience. This directional prediction is consistent with existing findings in contemplative neuroscience (reduced DMN activity, increased experiential openness) but would require more precise experimental design to rigorously test.

## 第六节：人本主义、超个人心理学与积极心理学——自我实现与解脱的根本分歧

### 一、马斯洛与罗杰斯：自我实现的预设

马斯洛（Abraham Maslow，1908–1970）的需求层次理论与自我实现（self-actualization）概念，以及罗杰斯（Carl Rogers，1902–1987）的以人为中心疗法（person-centered therapy），共同构成了人本主义心理学的核心。³¹

自我实现与转依（āśraya-parāvṛtti）之间的根本哲学对立，已在上一版论文中有所讨论，此处聚焦于几个新的分析角度：

**无条件积极关注与无条件慈悲**：罗杰斯的治疗关系三要素（无条件积极关注、共情、真诚），与佛教的慈悲观（karuṇā）有功能上的对应——两者都是以无执判的接纳态度面对来访者/众生。然而，罗杰斯的无条件积极关注仍然在治疗关系的框架内运作，是治疗师有意识的专业态度；佛教的无条件慈悲，建立在对一切众生皆有受（vedanā）、皆在无明苦恼中的深层认识之上，是从根本洞见中自然流露的存在性态度，而非专业技能的运用。³²

**马斯洛的高峰体验与唯识修行状态**：马斯洛晚年识别的”高峰体验”（peak experiences）——短暂的、仿佛超越自我边界的最优体验——与佛教禅定中的某些境界有表面相似性。但马斯洛的高峰体验是偶然发生的、无法系统培养的、也不带来持久的存在性转化；唯识修行通过系统的止观训练，旨在培养**稳定的**、**可复现的**、且导向**永久性转依**的意识转化——这是两者最根本的区别。


> **EN对应 · S6.1 Maslow and Rogers: The Presuppositions of Self-Actualization**
>
> Maslow’s (1908–1970) needs hierarchy and self-actualization concept, and Rogers’ (1902–1987) person-centered therapy, together constitute the core of humanistic psychology.³²
>
> The deepest philosophical opposition between self-actualization and āśraya-parāvṛtti: Maslow’s self-actualization presupposes a genuinely existing, waiting-to-be-realized “self” — self-actualization is the full unfolding of an already-present potential self. Even in Maslow’s transpersonal extension, “peak experiences” represent temporary expansions of consciousness, not fundamental recognitions of the nature of mind.
>
> Yogācāra practice fundamentally challenges this presupposition: the “self” continuously grasped by manas as an independent, substantial reality is, at the parikalpita level, a fabrication; practice aims not at realizing this self but at precisely recognizing its fabricated character (anātman), releasing the grasping, and allowing the nature of vijñāna (great-mirror wisdom) to manifest naturally.³³
>
> The practical implication: from Yogācāra’s perspective, Maslow’s self-actualization operates at the level of the imagined nature — valuable at the paratantra level, but if it does not proceed further toward the recognition of pariniṣpanna-svabhāva (the emptiness of self), it ultimately operates within the framework of self-grasping, unable to achieve genuine liberation.
>
> **Rogers’ unconditional positive regard and unconditional compassion**: Rogers’ three core therapeutic conditions (unconditional positive regard, empathy, congruence) share a functional correspondence with Buddhist compassion (karuṇā) — both involve non-judgmental, accepting encounter. Yet Rogers’ unconditional positive regard is a deliberate professional attitude within the therapeutic relationship; Buddhist unconditional compassion is an existential orientation naturally arising from the deep recognition that all sentient beings possess vedanā and are enmeshed in ignorance-driven suffering — grounded not in professional technique but in ontological insight.
### 二、超个人心理学：整合尝试的成就与局限

Ken Wilber 的”整合理论”（Integral Theory）试图建立一个能够容纳所有心理学传统（从行为主义到超个人）以及东西方灵性传统的统一框架，以”全象限全层次”（AQAL）模型为核心。³³

Wilber 的”前/超谬误”（pre/trans fallacy）分析——区分前个人（pre-personal）退行与超个人（transpersonal）发展，批判将婴儿期的自我-世界无分化与成熟的非二元觉知（nondual awareness）混为一谈³⁴——对理解唯识学与心理学的关系具有重要的方法论价值：它提示我们区分”自我边界的暂时弱化”（可能是退行）与”对自我虚妄性的洞见”（是修行成就），这两者在现象上可能相似，但存在论性质根本不同。

然而，Evan Thompson 在《我为何不是佛教徒》（*Why I Am Not a Buddhist*, 2020）中对超个人心理学整合尝试的批判值得认真对待：Wilber 的框架倾向于以西方进化发展论的目的论预设来解读佛教修行，将修行成就重新描述为”发展阶段”的达成，这在相当程度上遮蔽了佛教修行论真正的哲学深度。³⁵


> **EN对应 · S6.2 Transpersonal Psychology: The Achievement and Limits of Integration**
>
> Ken Wilber’s Integral Theory attempts a framework capable of accommodating all psychological traditions (from behaviorism to transpersonal) and Eastern and Western spiritual traditions, centered on his “All Quadrants, All Levels” (AQAL) model.³⁴
>
> Wilber’s “pre/trans fallacy” analysis — distinguishing pre-personal regression from transpersonal development, and critiquing the conflation of infantile ego-world merger with mature nondual awareness³⁵ — carries significant methodological value for Yogācāra-psychology comparison. It alerts us to distinguish “temporary weakening of ego boundaries” (which may represent regression) from “insight into the fabricated nature of self” (which is contemplative achievement) — two states that may appear phenomenologically similar but are ontologically different.
>
> However, Evan Thompson’s critique in *Why I Am Not a Buddhist* (2020) deserves serious engagement: Wilber’s framework tends to interpret Buddhist practice through the teleological presuppositions of Western evolutionary developmentalism, redescribing contemplative attainments as achievements of “developmental stages” — an interpretive move that substantially obscures the genuine philosophical depth of Buddhist soteriology.³⁶
### 三、积极心理学：蓬勃发展与苦恼根源

**Csikszentmihalyi 的心流与三摩地**：契克森米哈伊的”心流”（flow）描述了人在全神贯注于具有适当挑战性的活动时所经历的最优体验——自我意识消失、时间感改变、完全专注于当下活动。³⁶ 这与唯识学的三摩地（samādhi）有表面相似性，但根本差异在于：心流是功能性状态，在特定外部活动条件下自然产生；三摩地是修行成就，服务于对心灵本性的深层洞察。更重要的是，三摩地本身在唯识修行中不是终点，而是实现转依的前提条件。

**自我慈悲研究与唯识的慈悲观**：克里斯廷·内夫（Kristin Neff）发展的**自我慈悲**（self-compassion）研究³⁷，识别了自我慈悲的三个成分：自我友善（self-kindness）、共同人性（common humanity）与正念（mindfulness）。她的研究表明，自我慈悲比自尊（self-esteem）更能稳定地预测心理健康结果，且不带有自尊的比较性倾向。

这与唯识学的修行论有深刻的对话空间：唯识学强调末那识我执是以自我为中心的苦恼根源，而内夫的”共同人性”——认识到苦难是人类共同经验而非个人失败——正是在打破末那识我执导致的孤立自我感；正念成分则是培养对心理过程的去执观察能力。然而，差异在于：内夫的自我慈悲仍然以”自我”的健康发展为目标，是在改善自我与自我的关系；唯识修行最终指向的是对”自我”这一建构的根本超越（无我）。

**保罗·吉尔伯特（Paul Gilbert）的慈悲聚焦疗法（CFT）**：基于进化心理学（特别是三大情绪调节系统：威胁系统、驱动系统与安抚系统）与神经科学，CFT通过培养慈悲心（compassion）来帮助来访者激活”安抚系统”（soothing system），降低慢性羞耻感与自我批评。³⁸

CFT的安抚系统概念——以温暖、安全感与满足感为特征，与催产素-阿片系统相关联——与唯识学的修行进程有有趣的神经层面对应：禅定修行中培养的安住感（samatha的效果），在神经层面可能对应于安抚系统的激活；慈悲观修行，则在此基础上进一步培养对一切有情苦恼的共鸣性回应。这是一个值得进一步研究的”相互启发”方向。

> **EN对应 · S6.3 Positive Psychology: Flourishing, Flow, and the Limits of Optimism**
>
> **Csikszentmihalyi’s flow and samādhi**: the “flow” state — optimal experience characterized by loss of self-consciousness, altered time-sense, and complete present-focused absorption³⁷ — bears surface resemblance to Yogācāra’s account of samādhi (meditative concentration). But the fundamental difference is decisive: flow arises spontaneously under specific external activity conditions, requires no systematic training, and produces no lasting existential transformation; samādhi is a contemplative achievement cultivated through disciplined practice, serving as the necessary foundation for the deeper vipaśyanā observation that leads to āśraya-parāvṛtti.
>
> **Kristin Neff’s self-compassion and Yogācāra’s compassion practice**: Neff’s self-compassion framework³⁸ — with its three components of self-kindness, common humanity, and mindfulness — enters productive dialogue with Yogācāra’s treatment of manas-rooted self-isolation. The “common humanity” component — recognizing suffering as a universal human experience rather than a personal failure — directly counters the isolating function of the imagined-nature’s reification of a separate suffering self. The mindfulness component cultivates non-grasping observation of psychological processes. Yet Neff’s self-compassion ultimately aims at the healthy development of “self” and an improved relationship between self and self; Yogācāra practice ultimately points toward the fundamental transcendence of “self” as a reified construction — non-self (anātman). These are not identical goals.
>
> **Paul Gilbert’s Compassion-Focused Therapy (CFT)**: Gilbert’s CFT, grounded in evolutionary psychology and neuroscience, identifies three emotion-regulation systems (threat, drive, and soothing), employing compassion training to help clients activate the soothing system and reduce chronic shame and harsh self-criticism.³⁹ The soothing system — characterized by warmth, safeness, and contentment, associated with oxytocin-opioid systems — resonates with the bodily-mental ease (praśrabdhi) cultivated in śamatha practice, and the open, warm state developed in compassion meditation (karuṇā-bhāvanā). This constitutes a genuine case of cross-traditional mutual illumination, with implications for developing a neuroscience of contemplative compassion cultivation.

## 第七节：发展心理学、依恋理论与人际神经生物学——从关系到识流

### 一、依恋理论与熏习机制

约翰·鲍尔比（John Bowlby，1907–1990）的依恋理论，认为婴儿期与照顾者形成的依恋模式（安全型、焦虑型、回避型、混乱型），通过**内部工作模型**（internal working models）的建立，对终身的人际关系与心理健康有深远影响。³⁹

玛丽·安斯沃思（Mary Ainsworth）与玛丽·梅因（Mary Main）等人的后续研究，发展了依恋测量工具（陌生情境测试、成人依恋访谈），使依恋研究成为临床心理学中证据最为充分的领域之一。⁴⁰

依恋理论与唯识学的熏习机制，在”早期关系经验如何以持久方式影响认知、情感与行为倾向”这一结构上，有最为直接的对应关系：依恋理论的”内部工作模型”——对自我与他人的隐性期望与认知框架——在唯识学中对应于由早期关系经验熏习而成的**关系种子**（与我慢、我爱相关的特定种子），这些种子在未来的关系情境中持续现行，形成稳定的人际模式。

然而，唯识学提供了依恋理论所缺乏的更深层分析：依恋模式的固化，在唯识学看来，最终根植于末那识的我执——正是对独立自我的执取，使人在关系中持续寻求”自我”的确认、反映与保护。依恋安全感的建立（在治疗或良好关系中），在唯识学的框架中，相当于暂时弱化了某些特定关系种子的力量，但没有触及末那识我执的根本结构。这解释了为什么依恋治疗有时效果有限的深层原因。


> **EN对应 · S7.1 Attachment Theory and the Perfuming Mechanism**
>
> John Bowlby’s (1907–1990) attachment theory holds that the attachment patterns (secure, anxious, avoidant, disorganized) formed with caregivers in infancy, operating through **internal working models**, exert lasting influence on interpersonal relationships and psychological wellbeing throughout the lifespan.⁴⁰
>
> The structural correspondence with Yogācāra’s perfuming mechanism is the most direct in the entire comparative field: attachment theory’s internal working model — the implicit expectations and cognitive frameworks concerning self and other — corresponds, in Yogācāra’s analysis, to relational seeds formed through early relationship experience (particularly seeds connected with māna, ātmasneha, and ātmagrāha), which continuously manifest in future relational contexts, generating stable interpersonal patterns.
>
> Yogācāra provides a deeper analytical level that attachment theory lacks: the persistence of attachment patterns is rooted, ultimately, in manas’ self-grasping — it is the grasping at an independent self that causes people to continuously seek self-confirmation, mirroring, and protection in relationships. Therapeutic improvement of attachment patterns (through therapy or supportive relationships) amounts, in Yogācāra’s framework, to temporarily weakening certain specific relational seeds, without touching the structural self-grasping of manas. This explains the limited efficacy that attachment-based interventions sometimes demonstrate — they address the surface patterns without the root.
### 二、丹尼尔·西格尔的人际神经生物学：大脑、心灵与关系

西格尔（Daniel Siegel）发展的**人际神经生物学**（Interpersonal Neurobiology），整合了依恋理论、神经科学与正念研究，提出心灵本质上是”关系的与体化的能量与信息流的自我组织过程”。⁴¹ 他的”整合”（integration）概念——协调各种系统的分化部分为一个功能性整体，而不失去每个部分的独特性——成为其理解心理健康的核心。

西格尔的**反思功能**（reflective function）或**心智化**（mentalization）概念，与唯识学的修行机制有深刻的对话空间：反思功能是对自身与他人心理状态（意图、信念、情感）进行准确认识的能力，对依恋安全与心理健康至关重要。这与唯识学修行中对心理过程的如实观察（观，vipaśyanā），在功能层面有对应性——两者都强调对心理过程的元认知觉察。差异在于：反思功能的培养是通过关系体验（特别是治疗关系）实现的，唯识学的观是通过系统的修行实践实现的；反思功能仍然预设了一个进行反思的”自我”，唯识学的观最终指向认识到这个”反思的自我”本身也是识变的产物。


> **EN对应 · S7.2 Daniel Siegel’s Interpersonal Neurobiology**
>
> Siegel’s Interpersonal Neurobiology integrates attachment theory, neuroscience, and mindfulness research, defining mind as “a relational and embodied process that regulates the flow of energy and information.”⁴¹ His **integration** concept — the coordination of differentiated components into a functional whole without losing their distinctiveness — serves as his core framework for psychological health.
>
> Siegel’s **reflective function** or **mentalization** — the capacity to accurately represent one’s own and others’ mental states (intentions, beliefs, emotions) — enters productive dialogue with Yogācāra’s vipaśyanā mechanism: both emphasize metacognitive awareness of psychological processes as central to health and transformation. The difference: reflective function develops through relational experience (particularly the therapeutic relationship); Yogācāra’s vipaśyanā is cultivated through systematic contemplative practice; and reflective function still presupposes a self performing the reflection, while Yogācāra’s insight ultimately recognizes the “reflecting self” as itself a product of vijñāna-transformation.
### 三、表观遗传学与跨代际创伤：业力的生物学平行？

近年来表观遗传学（epigenetics）的发现，为唯识学的业力理论提供了一个最出乎意料的生物学平行：研究表明，强烈的心理创伤体验可以通过**表观遗传修饰**（epigenetic modifications）影响基因的表达方式，这些修饰有时可以**跨代际传递**，使创伤的影响传递给下一代乃至下下代，即便后代从未经历相同的创伤事件。⁴²

这与唯识学的业力种子理论有惊人的结构对应：**强烈的（特别是苦受色调强的）经验，在阿赖耶识中留下深厚的种子，这些种子通过轮回在未来生命中继续现行**——这在生物学层面有了一个（尽管只是部分的）可观察的平行。

然而，需要指出的是：表观遗传修饰的跨代际传递是生物学机制，操作于基因表达层面，是第三人称可观察的物质过程；唯识学的种子-业力机制是第一人称的识流过程，有感受性（vedanā）维度，不能被生物学机制所穷尽。这是一个有价值的结构同构，不是直接实证对应。

> **EN对应 · S7.3 Epigenetics and Transgenerational Trauma: A Biological Parallel to Karma?**
>
> Recent epigenetic research — demonstrating that severe psychological trauma can produce **epigenetic modifications** that are sometimes **transgenerationally transmissible**⁴² — provides one of the most surprising structural parallels to Yogācāra’s karma-seed theory: powerful experiences, particularly those with strong painful feeling-tone, leave impressions that can persist across generations, influencing psychological tendencies even in those who never directly experienced the original trauma.
>
> The structural correspondence with Yogācāra’s account is striking: seeds perfumed by intense (especially pain-toned) experience in the ālayavijñāna continue to manifest across lifetimes through the continuation of the vijñāna-stream. The epigenetic mechanism operates at the biological level of gene expression — a third-person observable material process; Yogācāra’s karma-seed mechanism operates within the first-person vijñāna-stream and has a vedanā dimension that cannot be exhausted by biological description. This is genuine structural homology, not direct empirical correspondence — but it is among the most striking such homologies across the entire comparative field.

## 第八节：情感神经科学、躯体心理学与创伤研究——受与身体的深层对话

### 一、潘克塞普的情感神经科学：七大基本情绪系统与烦恼心所

> **术语格义 · 烦恼心所（kleśa-caitta）**
> **梵** kleśa（烦恼）· caitta（心所）→ kleśa-caitta

雅克·潘克塞普（Jaak Panksepp，1943–2017）发展的**情感神经科学**（Affective Neuroscience），通过系统的动物与人类研究，识别了七个**基本情绪系统**（primary emotional systems）：**SEEKING**（探寻/欲望系统）、**RAGE**（愤怒系统）、**FEAR**（恐惧系统）、**LUST**（性欲系统）、**CARE**（养育系统）、**PANIC/GRIEF**（恐慌/悲痛系统）、**PLAY**（玩耍系统）。⁴³ 这些系统有其特定的神经回路（主要在皮层下脑区），是哺乳类动物共有的情感能力，与人类的高级认知功能相对独立。

潘克塞普的七大情绪系统与唯识学的烦恼心所（kleśa-caitta）分类，形成了迄今比较研究中最为精密的神经科学-唯识学对应关系。这是本文的原创性贡献之一：

**贪（lobha）** 与 **SEEKING 系统**：SEEKING 系统对应多巴胺（dopamine）能回路，驱动有机体寻求奖励、探索环境；唯识学的贪（lobha）是对令人愉悦的对象的执取性欲求。两者都描述了一种向外抓取、追求满足的基本驱动力，其神经基础高度重叠（Berridge 与 Robinson 的”欲望”vs.”喜好”区分，进一步精化了这一对应）。⁴⁴

**嗔（dveṣa）** 与 **RAGE 系统**：RAGE 系统在挫折或受到控制时激活，对应内侧丘脑-下丘脑-中脑导水管周围灰质回路；唯识学的嗔（dveṣa）是对不令人愉悦的对象的排斥性反应。两者的神经-识学对应是最为直接的。

**痴（moha）/无明（avidyā）** 的神经基础是最复杂的——它不对应潘克塞普任何单一的情绪系统，而是一种横贯所有情绪系统的认知模式（对诸法实相的根本不知）。这提示了唯识学无明概念的独特性：它不是一种情绪，而是一种认识论条件，使所有其他烦恼成为可能。

**慢（māna）** 与自我参照性神经处理：慢（māna，傲慢/比较性执取）没有直接的潘克塞普情绪系统对应，但与神经科学中的**自我参照性处理**（self-referential processing，涉及内侧前额叶皮层和后扣带回皮层）高度相关——这正是默认模式网络（DMN）的核心功能区域，与末那识的持续性我执形成了之前讨论过的神经相关性。

**疑（vicikitsā）** 与 **PANIC/GRIEF 系统**的某些面向：疑（vicikitsā，不安定的犹豫）在神经层面可能对应于前扣带回皮层（anterior cingulate cortex）的冲突监测功能，以及PANIC系统的激活（当缺乏可依赖的安全基础时产生的分离焦虑）。

这一分析虽然仍属于”结构同构”与”相互启发”之间的层次，但比以往任何比较研究都更为精确，为设计具体的神经影像学研究提供了操作化假设。


> **EN对应 · S8.1 Panksepp’s Affective Neuroscience: Seven Primary Systems and Afflictive Mental Factors**
>
> Jaak Panksepp’s (1943–2017) affective neuroscience, through systematic animal and human research, identified seven **primary emotional systems**: **SEEKING** (seeking/desire), **RAGE** (anger), **FEAR** (fear), **LUST** (sexuality), **CARE** (nurturance), **PANIC/GRIEF** (panic/grief), and **PLAY** (play).⁴³ These systems have specific neural circuits (primarily in subcortical brain regions), are shared across mammals, and operate relatively independently of higher cognitive functions.
>
> The correspondence between Panksepp’s seven primary systems and Yogācāra’s afflictive mental factors constitutes the most precise neuroscience-Yogācāra mapping yet identified. This is among this paper’s original contributions:
>
> **Lobha (greed) and the SEEKING system**: the SEEKING system, driven by dopaminergic circuits, drives organisms toward reward and exploration; lobha is the appropriative, grasping desire for pleasurable objects. Both describe a fundamental outward-reaching, satisfaction-pursuing drive with substantially overlapping neural substrates. Berridge and Robinson’s “wanting” versus “liking” distinction⁴⁴ further refines this correspondence — lobha maps more precisely onto “wanting” (SEEKING/dopaminergic) than onto “liking” (opioid-hedonic), consistent with Yogācāra’s analysis of greed as an appropriative orientation that generates its own perpetuation.
>
> **Dveṣa (aversion) and the RAGE system**: the RAGE system activates under frustration or constraint, corresponding to medial hypothalamo-thalamic-periaqueductal gray circuits; dveṣa is the rejection-oriented response to unpleasant objects. This is the most direct neural-Yogācāra correspondence of the set.
>
> **Moha (delusion) / avidyā (fundamental ignorance)**: most complex, this does not correspond to any single Panksepp system but constitutes a transversal cognitive condition (fundamental non-knowing of the nature of dharmas) that enables all other afflictions. This underscores the distinctiveness of the Yogācāra concept: avidyā is not an emotion but an epistemological condition, the enabling condition of all other kleśas.
>
> **Māna (conceit) and self-referential neural processing**: māna (comparative self-evaluative grasping) lacks direct correspondence in Panksepp’s system but correlates strongly with neuroscience’s **self-referential processing** — involving the medial prefrontal cortex and posterior cingulate cortex, core regions of the Default Mode Network (DMN). This connects to the manas-DMN correspondence developed below.
>
> This analysis remains at the level between structural homology and mutual illumination, but it is more precise than any previous comparative treatment, and generates operationalizable experimental hypotheses for neuroimaging research.
### 二、波雷斯的多元迷走神经理论：安全感的生理基础与修行的身体维度

> **主题对读注 · 多元迷走神经理论与修行之安全感维度**
> 本文第八节二以波雷斯（S. Porges）多元迷走神经理论的“神经觉知”（neuroception）与安全连接状态，对照戒律/安那般那/慈悲修持中“安全感”的身心前提，属“相互启发”型对话——不作生理学还原，而作为修行条件学（解读）的互参。
> 〔备考〕心所“轻安（praśrabdhi）”与副交感“安全”状态的对应关系宜标注〔待核〕，留待神经佛学（contemplative neuroscience）实证研究。

斯蒂芬·波雷斯（Stephen Porges）的**多元迷走神经理论**（Polyvagal Theory），提出自主神经系统有三个进化层次：**腹侧迷走神经复合体**（ventral vagal complex，VVC，对应安全感、社会联结状态）、**交感神经系统**（SNS，对应战斗/逃跑反应）与**背侧迷走神经复合体**（dorsal vagal complex，DVC，对应冻结/崩溃状态）。⁴⁵

这与唯识学的修行论形成了目前最具临床意义的跨传统对话：

唯识修行的基础条件之一，是**心理的安稳与适宜的外部环境**（亲近善知识，安住远离的处所）。波雷斯的理论提供了这一要求的神经生理学解释：**修行需要腹侧迷走神经系统的激活**——只有在感到足够安全（腹侧VVC激活）的状态下，高级的皮层整合功能（前额叶）才能正常工作，内省与洞察才成为可能；在威胁感知的状态下（SNS或DVC激活），系统进入防御模式，深度的修行内省几乎不可能发生。

这解释了为什么传统上强调修行环境的安静与安全，为什么治疗关系中的安全感（波雷斯称为”神经塑造”neurogenic safety）是治疗能够深入的前提，也为当代创伤知情的冥想教学提供了理论基础——对于有创伤历史的人，标准的正念指导可能触发防御性激活（SNS或DVC），而不是培养稳定的觉察能力。

此外，波雷斯理论中的**社会联结系统**（social engagement system）——腹侧VVC与面部、声音、听觉系统的连接，使安全感通过人际接触传递——与唯识学的共相种子理论有有趣的互证：人类通过有意义的社会互动（共业）在对方的识流中熏习共相种子，而这一过程，在神经生理学层面，正是通过腹侧迷走神经系统介导的社会联结功能实现的。


> **EN对应 · S8.2 Porges’ Polyvagal Theory: The Physiological Basis of Safety and Contemplative Practice**
>
> Stephen Porges’ **Polyvagal Theory** proposes that the autonomic nervous system has three evolutionary levels: the **ventral vagal complex** (VVC, corresponding to safety and social engagement), the **sympathetic nervous system** (SNS, fight/flight), and the **dorsal vagal complex** (DVC, freeze/collapse).⁴⁵
>
> This enters the most clinically significant cross-traditional dialogue available in the current comparative literature:
>
> One of the foundational conditions for Yogācāra practice is **psychological stability and appropriate external environment** (proximity to a learned teacher, dwelling in a suitable retreat setting). Polyvagal Theory provides the neurophysiological explanation: **contemplative practice requires ventral vagal complex activation**. Only in a state of sufficient safety (VVC activation) can higher cortical integration (prefrontal function) operate normally, enabling the introspection and insight that practice requires; in states of threat-detection (SNS or DVC activation), the system enters defensive mode, making deep contemplative introspection nearly impossible.
>
> This explains why tradition consistently emphasizes the importance of quiet, safe practice environments; why the safety of the therapeutic relationship is prerequisite for deep therapeutic work; and provides the theoretical foundation for trauma-informed contemplative teaching — for individuals with trauma histories, standard mindfulness instructions may inadvertently activate defensive responses (SNS or DVC) rather than cultivating stable awareness.
>
> Porges’ **social engagement system** — the VVC’s connection with facial, vocal, and auditory systems, enabling the transmission of safety through interpersonal contact — resonates with the shared-seeds theory: meaningful social interaction (shared karma) perfumes shared seeds in others’ vijñāna-streams, and this process is mediated, at the neurophysiological level, precisely by the social engagement function of the ventral vagal system.
### 三、创伤研究：范德科尔克与利文的躯体取向

贝塞尔·范德科尔克（Bessel van der Kolk）在《身体记录了创伤》（*The Body Keeps the Score*, 2014）中，综合神经科学、依恋理论与躯体心理学，论证了创伤不只是”心理”事件，而是深刻改变了身体、大脑与心理感知的整体性经验，且其影响在身体层面留下了无法通过认知干预直接触及的印记。⁴⁶

这与唯识学的有根身（sāśraya）理论形成了深刻的对话空间：在唯识学的框架中，强烈的苦受体验（创伤事件）通过意识与身体的密切联系，在阿赖耶识中留下带有极重苦受色调的种子，这些种子通过有根身（阿赖耶识所执受的身体）产生身体层面的记忆与反应。范德科尔克的研究在神经层面印证了这一机制的某个层面：创伤记忆以**感觉运动模式**储存（而非叙事性记忆），激活时绕过海马体（通常负责叙事记忆的整合）直接激活杏仁核，产生”身体知道但语言不知道”的创伤反应。

唯识学的分析提供了更深层的理解框架：创伤的持久性，不只是神经回路的固化，更是有受的业力印记在识流中的持续，这种持续有感受性（vedanā）维度，不能被单纯的认知或神经干预所穷尽，需要通过改变与这些经验的关系（正念觉察，最终指向转依）才能产生根本性的改变。

彼得·利文（Peter Levine）的**躯体体验**（Somatic Experiencing）疗法，通过关注身体感觉与本能反应来处理创伤的神经系统影响，特别强调”完成”未完成的防御反应以释放冻结的神经能量。⁴⁷ 这与唯识学修行论中的身（kāya）与受（vedanā）层面的修行（如念处修行中的身受心法观）有功能上的对应：两者都承认身体层面的修行对心理转化的不可或缺性，都强调对感受（feeling/vedanā）的精密觉察是转化的关键。

>
> Bessel van der Kolk’s *The Body Keeps the Score* (2014) synthesizes neuroscience, attachment theory, and somatic psychology to argue that trauma is not merely a “psychological” event but profoundly alters body, brain, and perceptual processes, leaving impressions at the bodily level inaccessible to purely cognitive intervention.⁴⁶
>

Van der Kolk’s neuroscientific findings are partially consonant: traumatic memories are stored as **sensorimotor patterns** (rather than narrative memories), bypassing the hippocampus to directly activate the amygdala, producing “the body knows what language doesn’t.”

> Peter Levine’s **Somatic Experiencing** (SE) therapy addresses trauma through tracking bodily sensation and instinctual responses, emphasizing the “completion” of incomplete defensive responses to release frozen neural energy.⁴⁷ This corresponds functionally with certain dimensions of Yogācāra’s body-sensation practice (the mindfulness of body and vedanā in the four foundations of mindfulness): both recognize the indispensability of somatic engagement to psychological transformation, both emphasize precise awareness of feeling (feeling/vedanā) as the key to transformation.

## 第九节：社会心理学、文化心理学与批判心理学

### 一、社会心理学：自我构建的集体维度

社会心理学对自我构建的研究，特别是关于**社会认同**（social identity）与**自我服务偏见**（self-serving bias）的发现，与唯识学的末那识我执理论形成了重要的补充与对话：

海尔·泰弗尔（Henri Tajfel）与约翰·特纳（John Turner）的**社会认同理论**（Social Identity Theory），论证了人类倾向于将自身认同与所属群体（内群体）联系起来，并对内群体产生偏爱，对外群体产生偏见（内外群体偏见，in-group/out-group bias）。⁴⁸ 这与唯识学末那识我执的分析高度呼应：末那识不只是执取个体层面的”我”，也通过认同（我慢）执取各种集体身份（民族、国家、宗教、阶级）为”我”的延伸。末那识的我执，因此不只是个体心理学问题，也是社会心理学问题。

**认知失调理论**（Leon Festinger）——描述了人类如何在持有不一致信念时产生心理紧张并寻求协调——与唯识学的遍计所执对一致性的执取有结构上的共鸣；两者都揭示了人类心理对内在一致性（即便是虚妄的一致性）的强烈偏好。


> **EN对应 · S9.1 Social Psychology: The Collective Dimension of Self-Construction**
>
> Henri Tajfel and John Turner’s **Social Identity Theory** demonstrates that human beings systematically identify with their group memberships (in-group), generating in-group favoritism and out-group prejudice.⁴⁸ This directly parallels Yogācāra’s analysis of manas-rooted self-grasping: manas does not only grasp individual “I”-ness; through māna (conceit/comparison), it grasps various collective identities (nation, religion, class) as extensions of “self.” Manas’ self-grasping is thus not only an individual-psychological problem but a social-psychological one — with profound implications for understanding collective conflict, ideological violence, and the psychology of in-group solidarity.
>
> Leon Festinger’s **cognitive dissonance theory** — describing the psychological tension humans experience when holding inconsistent beliefs and their motivated pursuit of consistency⁴⁹ — resonates with Yogācāra’s account of the imagined nature’s commitment to consistency: both reveal that the human mind shows a strong bias toward internal coherence, even when this coherence is a fabrication.
### 二、文化心理学：普遍性与文化特殊性

理查德·施韦德（Richard Shweder）的文化心理学，以及特里安迪斯（Harry Triandis）等人关于**个人主义-集体主义**维度的跨文化研究，提示了心理学概念的文化特殊性。⁴⁹ 很多西方心理学的”普遍规律”，在跨文化检验中表现出显著的文化变异性。

这对唯识学与西方心理学的比较具有重要的方法论含义：西方心理学（特别是精神分析与人本主义）发展于特定的个人主义文化背景下，其关于”自我”、”自主性”、”自我实现”的预设，在很大程度上是文化特定的，而非普遍人类心理的反映。唯识学的某些洞见——特别是关于末那识我执的分析——提供了一个批判性地审视西方个人主义心理学文化预设的理论资源。

Mark Siderits 在《佛教与哲学》（2007）中指出，无我论（anātman）不只是形而上学主张，也是对西方心理学个人主义自我概念的哲学批判，这一批判在文化心理学的跨文化研究中找到了经验支持。⁵⁰


> **EN对应 · S9.2 Cultural Psychology and the Cultural Specificity of “Self”**
>
> Richard Shweder’s cultural psychology and Triandis’ cross-cultural research on **individualism-collectivism** have demonstrated that many apparently universal psychological “laws” show substantial cross-cultural variation.⁵⁰
>
> This carries important methodological implications for Yogācāra-psychology comparison: much of Western psychology (particularly psychoanalysis and humanism) developed within specific individualist cultural contexts, and its presuppositions about “self,” “autonomy,” and “self-actualization” are substantially culture-specific rather than universally human. Yogācāra’s analysis of manas self-grasping provides a critical resource for examining these cultural presuppositions — not because Yogācāra is culture-neutral (it has its own cultural and institutional contexts), but because its ontological critique of selfhood operates at a level deeper than cultural variation.
### 三、解放心理学：心理学作为解放工具

伊格纳西奥·马丁-巴罗（Ignacio Martín-Baró，1942–1989）发展的**解放心理学**（Liberation Psychology），批判传统心理学服务于维护现存社会秩序，呼吁心理学作为受压迫者解放的工具。⁵¹

这与唯识学的解脱论维度有深刻的精神共鸣，尽管两者的”解放”含义根本不同：唯识学的解脱（mokṣa）是从轮回苦恼的存在性束缚中解放，解放心理学的解放是从社会-政治压迫中解放。然而，两者共同指向了一个被主流西方心理学所遮蔽的维度：心理学不能只关注个体内在的适应与调节，必须面对心理苦恼的外在（社会）根源与内在（存在性）根源。

从唯识学的角度，解放心理学的视野可以被进一步深化：社会性的压迫（外在苦恼根源）与末那识我执（内在苦恼根源）是相互强化的——在充满竞争与压迫的社会结构中，末那识我执得到更强的熏习；而末那识我执使人以自我中心的方式参与社会关系，为不公正结构的再生产提供了心理基础。真正的解放，需要同时面对外在的社会结构变革与内在的末那识我执对治。

> **EN对应 · S9.3 Liberation Psychology: Psychology as a Tool for Emancipation**
>
> Ignacio Martín-Baró’s (1942–1989) **Liberation Psychology** critiques traditional psychology for serving to maintain existing social orders, calling for psychology as an instrument of liberation for the oppressed.⁵¹
>
> The spiritual resonance with Yogācāra’s soteriological orientation is genuine, though the referents of “liberation” differ fundamentally: Yogācāra’s liberation (mokṣa) is from the existential bondage of karma-driven conditioned existence; Liberation Psychology’s liberation is from socio-political oppression. But both point toward a dimension obscured by mainstream Western psychology: psychological suffering has both external (social) and internal (existential) sources that cannot be addressed by purely inward-oriented adjustment. Yogācāra’s perspective actually deepens Liberation Psychology’s analysis: social oppression (external suffering-source) and manas self-grasping (internal suffering-source) are mutually reinforcing — competitive and oppressive social structures provide intensified perfuming of self-grasping seeds; while self-grasping manas causes people to participate in social relationships in self-centered ways, providing psychological infrastructure for the reproduction of unjust structures. Genuine liberation requires simultaneously addressing both external structural transformation and the internal counter-working of manas self-grasping.

## 第十节：第三波认知行为治疗与修行传统的整合

> **主题对读注 · 第三波整合（MBSR/MBCT/ACT/CFT/DBT）与唯识**
> 本文第十节评估正念减压（MBSR）、正念认知疗法（MBCT）、接受与承诺治疗（ACT）、辩证行为治疗（DBT）、慈悲聚焦疗法（CFT）作为“内隐唯识”的开放程度：其共同预设（以观察者脱钩于被观察之念）与唯识“无我”分析相契，但在目的结构与自我观上保持根本差异；本节英文原句全文已由「EN对应」块逐小节给出。

### 一、正念减压（MBSR）与正念认知疗法（MBCT）

Jon Kabat-Zinn 于1979年创立的正念减压（MBSR），已在大量随机对照试验中证明对慢性疼痛、焦虑与抑郁的显著疗效。⁵² Segal、Williams 与 Teasdale 发展的正念认知疗法（MBCT），更在复发性抑郁症防止复发方面取得了国际临床指南级别的证据支持。⁵³

MBCT的核心机制是培养对思维与情绪的**元认知觉察**——能够以”思维只是思维，不是事实”的态度观察心理内容，而非被其牵着走。这与唯识修行中对识变过程的如实观察（不执取，不排斥）有深刻的方法论共鸣：两者都试图改变人与心理内容的关系——从认同转向观察，从被内容控制转向保持距离地感受内容的来去。

**然而，需要精确指出的是**：MBCT 的正念，是从南传佛教的 sati（念）修行传统中提取的世俗化技术，与唯识学的止观（śamatha-vipaśyanā）传统有所不同。唯识学的观（vipaśyanā），最终指向的是对识变机制的如实认识（”此所缘唯识所现”）与无我（anātman）的直接体证；MBCT 的正念，以元认知觉察与情绪调节为目标，不涉及这一解脱论层面。这不是对MBCT的贬低，而是精确标注其有效范围：MBCT 是依他起层面的心理干预；唯识修行服务于圆成实性的证悟。


> **EN对应 · S10.1 MBSR and MBCT**
>
> Jon Kabat-Zinn’s (1979) Mindfulness-Based Stress Reduction (MBSR) has demonstrated significant efficacy for chronic pain, anxiety, and depression in numerous randomized controlled trials.⁵² Segal, Williams, and Teasdale’s Mindfulness-Based Cognitive Therapy (MBCT) has achieved clinical guideline status for prevention of depressive relapse.⁵³
>
> MBCT’s core mechanism — cultivating **metacognitive awareness** in which thoughts can be observed with the attitude “thoughts are just thoughts, not facts,” without being swept along by them — resonates deeply with Yogācāra practice’s as-it-is observation of vijñāna-transformation processes (without grasping, without rejection). Both attempt to transform the person’s relationship to psychological contents — from identification to observation, from being controlled by contents to maintaining observational distance.
>
> **Critical precision is required, however.** MBCT’s mindfulness derives primarily from the Theravāda *sati* (mindfulness) tradition, differing from Yogācāra’s śamatha-vipaśyanā (calm-insight) in several respects. Yogācāra’s vipaśyanā ultimately aims at precise recognition of the vijñāna-transformation mechanism (”this perceived object is vijñāna’s own manifestation”) and direct realization of anātman; MBCT’s mindfulness aims at metacognitive awareness and emotion regulation, without reference to this soteriological level. This is not disparagement but precise delineation of scope: MBCT is a paratantra-level psychological intervention; Yogācāra practice serves the realization of pariniṣpanna-svabhāva.
### 二、接受与承诺治疗（ACT）与唯识三性

Steven Hayes 的 ACT，以心理灵活性（psychological flexibility）为核心目标，通过接受（acceptance）、去融合（defusion）、当下时刻（present moment）、观察自我（observing self）、价值（values）与承诺行动（committed action）六个核心过程，试图改变人与思维和感受的关系。⁵⁴

ACT 的”认知去融合”（cognitive defusion）技术，与唯识学三性说中从遍计所执（将所有心理内容执为实有）到依他起（如实认识心理内容的缘起性质）的转变，有功能上最为直接的对应关系：两者都试图使人与心理内容保持观察的距离，从”认同内容”转向”观察内容来去”，从”被内容控制”转向”主动选择行动方向”。

然而，关键的差异在于：ACT 的去融合，目标是增加心理灵活性以更有效地追求个人价值观导向的生活；唯识学从遍计执到依他起的转变，只是修行的一个阶段，其终极目标是圆成实性（空性）的体证，即对识之本性的彻底如实认识。ACT 没有唯识学的解脱论维度；唯识学所追求的，远不止于心理灵活性。

**ACT 的”观察自我”（observing self）与唯识的自证分**：ACT 提出了一个超越认同内容的”观察自我”视角——一个能够觉察自身思维与情绪而不与之融合的稳定观察点。这与唯识学的自证分（sva-saṃvedana）概念有值得深入探讨的结构对应：两者都指向一个在认识活动中不被认识内容所淹没的觉察维度。差异在于：ACT 的观察自我仍然是一种心理功能立场（a perspective），可以被培养为治疗技术；唯识学的自证分是识的基本结构特征，不是可以选择采用或不采用的立场，而是一切识活动的本质组成部分。


> **EN对应 · S10.2 Acceptance and Commitment Therapy (ACT) and the Three Natures**
>
> Steven Hayes’ ACT, with psychological flexibility as its central goal, employs six core processes — acceptance, defusion, present moment, observing self, values, and committed action — to change the person’s **relationship** with thoughts and feelings rather than their content.⁵⁴
>
> ACT’s **cognitive defusion** technique bears the most direct functional correspondence with Yogācāra’s shift from parikalpita-svabhāva (treating all psychological contents as inherently real) to paratantra-svabhāva (recognizing the dependent-arising nature of psychological contents): both attempt to enable persons to maintain observational distance from psychological content, moving from “identifying with content” to “watching content arise and pass,” from “being controlled by content” to “choosing action direction deliberately.”
>
> The critical difference: ACT’s defusion aims at increasing psychological flexibility to more effectively pursue values-directed living; Yogācāra’s shift from parikalpita to paratantra is only one stage, with the ultimate goal being the realization of pariniṣpanna-svabhāva (emptiness as the nature of dependent arising itself). ACT lacks the soteriological dimension; Yogācāra aims at far more than psychological flexibility.
>
> **ACT’s “observing self” and sva-saṃvedana**: ACT posits an “observing self” perspective — a stable point of awareness that can witness one’s own thoughts and emotions without fusion with them — which resonates with Yogācāra’s sva-saṃvedana (self-witnessing aspect). Both point toward an awareness dimension that is not overwhelmed by cognitive contents. The difference: ACT’s observing self is a functional perspective (a stance to be cultivated as a therapeutic skill); Yogācāra’s sva-saṃvedana is a structural feature of all vijñāna — not a perspective one can choose to adopt or not, but an intrinsic constitutive feature of every vijñāna-activity.
### 三、辩证行为治疗（DBT）与情绪调节

Marsha Linehan 的辩证行为治疗（DBT），主要用于边缘型人格障碍，以接受与改变的辩证整合为核心原则，包括正念（mindfulness）、痛苦忍受（distress tolerance）、情绪调节（emotion regulation）与人际效能（interpersonal effectiveness）四个技能模块。⁵⁵

DBT 的”彻底接受”（radical acceptance）——以完全、非评判的方式接受现实，不试图改变无法改变的东西——与唯识学的依他起性层面的如实观察有功能性的对应：两者都强调接受当下实际，不对其添加遍计执的实有化。

然而，DBT的”接受”是作为”改变的前提”而存在的，其最终目标是行为的改变与生活质量的提升；唯识学的如实接受（不执不舍），服务于对识之本性的更深层认识，其终极目标是存在结构的转变（转依）。


> **EN对应 · S10.3 Dialectical Behavior Therapy (DBT) and Emotional Regulation**
>
> Marsha Linehan’s Dialectical Behavior Therapy (DBT), centering on the dialectical integration of acceptance and change, includes four skill modules: mindfulness, distress tolerance, emotion regulation, and interpersonal effectiveness.⁵⁵
>
> DBT’s “radical acceptance” — accepting reality completely and non-judgmentally without trying to change what cannot be changed — corresponds functionally with Yogācāra’s as-is observation at the paratantra level: both emphasize accepting present reality without adding the imagined nature’s reification. The difference: DBT’s acceptance is the premise for change, its ultimate goal being improved functioning and quality of life; Yogācāra’s acceptance (non-grasping, non-rejection) serves deeper recognition of vijñāna’s nature, with liberation as the ultimate goal.
### 四、慈悲聚焦疗法（CFT）与内心批评者的对治

保罗·吉尔伯特（Paul Gilbert）的慈悲聚焦疗法（CFT），通过培养**慈悲心**（compassion for self and others）来平衡由进化遗留的威胁系统（threat system）的过度激活，特别针对慢性羞耻感与严苛自我批评。⁵⁶

CFT 的三大情绪调节系统（威胁、驱动、安抚）与波雷斯的多元迷走神经理论有深度整合，也与唯识学的烦恼与善心所的对应关系形成了多层面的对话：

CFT 的”威胁系统”（threat system）激活，对应于唯识学的嗔（dveṣa）与恐惧相关烦恼心所；”驱动系统”（drive system）激活，对应于贪（lobha）与寻求满足的SEEKING系统；”安抚系统”（soothing system）激活，对应于正念修行中禅定状态的身心轻安（praśrabdhi），以及慈悲观修行所培养的开放温暖状态。

CFT 的慈悲训练与唯识学的慈悲修行（karuṇā-bhāvanā），在培养对苦难的敏感性与回应意愿方面有功能上的对应。差异在于：CFT 的慈悲是进化框架内的情绪调节工具；唯识学的慈悲，建立在无我洞见之上（”无缘大慈，同体大悲”），是超越进化框架的存在性态度转变。


> **EN对应 · S10.4 Compassion-Focused Therapy (CFT) and the Cultivation of Karuṇā**
>
> Paul Gilbert’s CFT, employing compassion training to activate the soothing system and counter chronic shame and self-criticism,⁵⁶ yields a multi-level dialogue with Yogācāra:
>
> CFT’s threat-system activation corresponds to Yogācāra’s dveṣa and fear-related kleśas; drive-system activation corresponds to lobha and the SEEKING system; soothing-system activation corresponds to the bodily-mental ease (praśrabdhi) of śamatha, and the open warmth cultivated in compassion practice.
>
> CFT’s compassion training and Yogācāra’s karuṇā-bhāvanā share a functional orientation toward sensitivity to suffering and the motivation to respond. The critical difference: CFT’s compassion is an evolutionary emotion-regulation tool within the threat/drive/soothing system framework; Yogācāra’s karuṇā, grounded in non-self insight (”uncaused loving-kindness, compassion from shared being,” 無緣大慈同體大悲), is an existential-attitudinal transformation transcending the evolutionary framework.
### 五、内在家庭系统疗法（IFS）：心灵的部分与识的活动

理查德·施瓦茨（Richard C. Schwartz）发展的**内在家庭系统疗法**（Internal Family Systems, IFS），提出心灵由多个”部分”（parts）组成，每个部分都有其特定的角色、信念与感受；心理健康的目标是从”自我”（Self，一种本质上清明、慈悲与智慧的意识状态）出发，与所有部分建立和谐的关系。⁵⁷

IFS 的”自我”（Self）概念——超越所有”部分”的清明觉察状态，具有好奇、慈悲、清明、连接、创造力、勇气、承诺与平静八个品质——与唯识学的修行目标有令人惊讶的结构对应：IFS 的 Self 与唯识学的转依后的识（大圆镜智、平等性智等四智），都描述了一种从执取与防御中解放出来的清明意识状态。

差异在于：IFS 将 Self 理解为每个人本来就具有的心理资源，通过治疗可以使之与各个部分建立联系；唯识学认为这种清明觉察（识之本性）需要通过系统的修行实践（特别是对末那识我执的根本对治）才能稳定显现，且其显现意味着对”部分”（即遍计所执的各种心所执取）的根本超越，而非与之建立”和谐关系”。

---


> **EN对应 · S10.5 Internal Family Systems (IFS) and the Multiplicity of Mind**
>
> Richard Schwartz’s Internal Family Systems therapy proposes that the mind comprises multiple “parts,” each with specific roles, beliefs, and feelings, with psychological health achieved when the “Self” (a fundamentally clear, compassionate, and wise state of awareness) relates harmoniously to all parts.⁵⁷
>
> IFS’s “Self” — transcending all “parts” with curiosity, compassion, clarity, connection, creativity, courage, commitment, and calmness — exhibits a structurally surprising resonance with Yogācāra’s account of āśraya-parāvṛtti: IFS’s Self and the four wisdoms of transformed vijñāna (great-mirror wisdom, wisdom of equality, etc.) both describe a state of lucid awareness liberated from grasping and defensive reactivity.
>
> The difference: IFS treats Self as a psychological resource already present in each person, accessible through therapy, capable of establishing harmonious relations with all parts; Yogācāra holds that this lucid awareness (the nature of vijñāna itself) manifests stably only through systematic practice — particularly the fundamental countering of manas’ self-grasping — and that its manifestation means a fundamental transcendence of “parts” (the various kleśic grasping of the imagined nature), not merely the establishment of “harmonious relations” with them.

### 一、预测编码与自由能原理的深化分析

前面已在第五节中介绍了预测处理理论与唯识学的基本对话。此处深化若干关键点：

**Karl Friston 的自由能原理（Free Energy Principle）**认为，生命系统通过最小化”惊奇”（surprise）或”自由能”（free energy）来维持自身的组织完整性——从最基本的细胞到最复杂的心灵，都在进行这种最小化过程。⁵⁸ 这提供了一个令人惊讶的视角来重新理解末那识：**末那识持续执取”自我”，可能是生命系统为了最小化”自我-模型的惊奇”而进行的计算性活动的心理表达**——维持一个稳定的自我模型，是预测处理框架下大脑活动的核心目标之一。

若如此，唯识学修行（通过观洞察末那识我执的虚妄性）相当于**系统性地挑战并更新大脑的自我生成模型**，使系统从”以固定自我模型最小化惊奇”转向”以更灵活、更精确的无我模型最小化惊奇”。这不是消除预测处理，而是根本性地更新生成模型。转依，在预测处理的框架中，可能对应于生成模型的最根本层次（关于”自我”的先验信念）的彻底更新。

这一分析有一个可测试的预测：相较于普通成人，实现了高度转依（如长期禅修者）的大脑，其自我参照性网络（DMN）应该表现出更灵活的、更低精密性的先验信念——即对”稳定自我存在”的预测更弱，对各种体验的接受更宽广。这与现有冥想神经科学的某些发现（DMN 活动减弱，对体验的开放性增加）方向一致，但需要更精确的实验设计来检验。


> **EN对应 · S11.1 The Free Energy Principle: A Deepened Analysis**
>
> Friston’s **Free Energy Principle** proposes that living systems maintain their organizational integrity by minimizing “surprise” or “free energy” — from the most basic cell to the most complex mind.⁵⁸ This offers a surprising perspective for reconceiving manas: **manas’ continuous self-grasping may be the psychological expression of the brain’s computational activity in minimizing “self-model surprise”** — maintaining a stable self-model is one of the central goals of predictive-processing brain activity.
>
> If so, Yogācāra contemplative practice (through vipaśyanā insight into the fabricated nature of manas’ self-grasping) amounts to **systematically challenging and updating the brain’s self-generative model**, transitioning the system from “minimizing surprise with a fixed self-model” to “minimizing surprise with a more flexible, more accurate non-self model.” This is not the elimination of predictive processing but the fundamental updating of the generative model. Āśraya-parāvṛtti, within the predictive processing framework, may correspond to the most fundamental level of generative model updating — complete revision of the prior beliefs concerning “self.”
>
> This analysis yields a testable prediction: compared with ordinary adults, practitioners who have realized advanced āśraya-parāvṛtti should show more flexible, lower-precision prior beliefs in their self-referential networks — weaker predictions of “stable self-existence,” broader receptivity to varied experience. This directional prediction is consistent with existing contemplative neuroscience findings but requires more precisely designed experimental paradigms to rigorously test.
### 二、4E认知科学与有根身

当代认知科学的”4E”革命——**具身**（embodied）、**嵌入**（embedded）、**生成**（enacted）、**延展**（extended）认知——从多个维度挑战了传统的计算-表征认知模型，强调心灵不是孤立大脑中的信息处理，而是身体、环境与社会文化的共同生成。⁵⁹

这与唯识学的识变论形成了多层面的对话：

**具身认知与有根身**：4E认知强调身体（包括感觉运动经验）对认知内容的构成性影响；唯识学的阿赖耶识执受有根身，强调身体不是独立于识的物质基础，而是识在特定业力条件下的凝结形态——这是比4E认知更根本的声明，因为它不只是说身体影响认知，而是说身体本身是识变的产物。

**延展心灵与共相种子**：延展心灵理论（Clark & Chalmers）认为心理过程可以延展到颅骨之外，包括环境中的物体与他人；唯识学的共相种子理论，以更系统的方式说明了心理现实如何是众生识流共同建构的——不是延展的个体心灵，而是无数识流通过共业共同变现的器世间。这是比延展心灵更为彻底的关系性与社会性。


> **EN对应 · S11.2 4E Cognitive Science and the Sentient Body**
>
> The 4E cognitive revolution — **embodied**, **embedded**, **enacted**, **extended** cognition — challenges the computational-representational model of cognition, emphasizing that mind is not information processing in an isolated brain but the joint generation of body, environment, and social culture.⁵⁹
>
> **Embodied cognition and sāśraya**: 4E cognition emphasizes the constitutive influence of bodily (especially sensorimotor) experience on cognitive content; Yogācāra’s ālayavijñāna grasping of the sentient body with its faculties makes a more fundamental claim — that the body is not an independent material substrate for vijñāna but a crystallized form of vijñāna under specific karmic conditions. This is more radical than 4E cognition: not merely that body influences cognition, but that the body itself is a product of vijñāna-transformation.
>
> **Extended mind and shared seeds**: the extended mind theory (Clark and Chalmers) holds that psychological processes can extend beyond the skull to include environmental objects and other persons. Yogācāra’s shared-seeds theory provides a more systematic account of how psychological reality is co-constituted by countless vijñāna-streams — not an extended individual mind but an intersubjective world jointly manifested by beings through shared karma. This represents a more thoroughgoing relationality and sociality than extended mind theory envisions.
### 三、迷幻剂辅助治疗：最意外的跨传统印证

近年来，随着美国 FDA 对致幻剂辅助治疗的相继批准（MDMA 辅助治疗 PTSD、氯胺酮治疗难治性抑郁），以及对裸盖菇素（psilocybin）和 LSD 的临床试验的推进，一个令人惊讶的现象进入了研究者的视野：**致幻剂诱发的神秘体验（mystical experiences），与佛教（特别是禅定与大圆满修行）的某些描述，存在惊人的现象学相似性**。⁶⁰

Robin Carhart-Harris 等人的研究发现，裸盖菇素通过抑制默认模式网络（DMN）的活动，产生”自我消融”（ego dissolution）的体验——边界感消失、与更大整体的融合感、时间体验的根本改变——这些描述与禅宗的”无我境界”及唯识学禅定深处的描述有显著的现象学重叠。⁶¹

然而，需要极为审慎地处理这一对应：

**相似性**：DMN 抑制导致的自我边界弱化，与禅定中末那识我执的暂时弱化，在现象学描述上有相似性；致幻剂体验中的”万物一体感”，与华严的法界缘起观在描述上有共鸣。

**根本差异**：致幻剂诱发的神秘体验是由外源性化学物质强制性地改变神经化学状态产生的，不涉及对心理机制的任何真实认识，也不产生持久的存在性转化（尽管某些研究显示对某些来访者的长期心理健康有益）；唯识修行的禅定，是通过系统的心灵训练培养起来的专注力，服务于对心灵本性的深层洞察（毗婆舍那），以实现持久的转依。从唯识学的角度，致幻剂体验最多是一种偶然的”见性”（使某些人对心灵本性有短暂的瞥见），但由于缺乏修行的基础与后续的整合，这种瞥见通常不能产生持久的转依。

**新的研究方向**：迷幻剂研究与唯识学的对话，最有价值的方向不是相互印证，而是相互提问：从唯识学的角度，是什么使某些迷幻剂体验在少数人身上产生了持久的积极变化？是否是因为这种体验触发了对遍计所执的短暂解除，使某些人看见了依他起的本来面目？从迷幻剂研究的角度，禅定修行的神经机制与致幻剂的神经机制（都涉及DMN抑制）有何异同？是什么使系统的修行训练产生更持久的效果？


> **EN对应 · S11.3 Psychedelic-Assisted Therapy: Unexpected Cross-Traditional Resonances**
>
> With FDA breakthrough designation for MDMA-assisted PTSD treatment and advancing clinical trials for psilocybin and LSD, a striking phenomenon has entered scientific attention: **the mystical experiences induced by psychedelics — ego dissolution, dissolution of self-other boundaries, fusion with a larger whole, radical alteration of temporal experience — show remarkable phenomenological similarity to descriptions in certain Buddhist contemplative traditions (particularly Chan enlightenment and Dzogchen practice)**.⁶⁰
>
> Robin Carhart-Harris and colleagues have found that psilocybin, by suppressing Default Mode Network (DMN) activity, produces **ego dissolution** experiences matching descriptions of Yogācāra’s meditative states in certain respects.⁶¹
>
> **Critical methodological caution is essential**, however:
>
> Psychedelic-induced mystical experiences are produced by exogenous chemical substances that forcibly alter neurochemical states — they involve no genuine recognition of the mechanisms of mind, and do not produce lasting existential transformation (though some studies show long-term psychological health benefits for certain individuals). Yogācāra’s contemplative concentration is a disciplined training in focused attention, serving the deep vipaśyanā insight that leads to āśraya-parāvṛtti.
>
> From Yogācāra’s perspective, psychedelic experiences may constitute at most a kind of accidental “glimpse” of the nature of mind (enabling some people to briefly perceive what an absence of habitual self-grasping might feel like), but lacking the contemplative foundation and subsequent integration, such glimpses typically cannot produce stable āśraya-parāvṛtti.
>
> **The most valuable research direction**: the dialogue between psychedelic research and Yogācāra lies not in mutual confirmation but in mutual questioning. From Yogācāra’s perspective: what causes certain psychedelic experiences to produce lasting positive changes in a small number of individuals? Is it because the experience briefly disables the imagined-nature’s reifications, enabling some people to glimpse the paratantra nature of things? From psychedelic research’s perspective: what distinguishes the neural mechanisms of sustained contemplative practice from those of psychedelic intervention (both involve DMN suppression)? What enables systematic contemplative training to produce more lasting effects?
### 四、神经多样性视角：重新思考”正常”与”异常”

**神经多样性**（neurodiversity）运动，以自闭症权利运动为起点，挑战了将神经发育差异（包括自闭症、ADHD、双相情感障碍等）视为需要被治疗的”缺陷”的医学模型，提出这些差异是人类神经多样性的自然体现，有其独特的价值与优势。⁶²

这与唯识学的视角形成了有趣的对话：唯识学并不以特定的神经发育模式或心理功能风格作为”正常”的标准；其对苦恼根源的分析（末那识我执），是跨越所有神经类型的共同处境，每一种神经类型都面临其特有形式的遍计所执，也都有其独特的觉察路径。唯识修行的传统，实际上高度尊重”根机”（gēnjī，个体的心理-精神能力与倾向）的多样性，不同根机的人需要不同的修行方法。

这一视角提示，对话不应该以西方心理学（建立在特定神经类型规范之上的）”健康”标准来衡量唯识修行的成就，而应该以对苦恼根源（末那识我执）的洞察程度来衡量，这一洞察与神经类型无关。

> **EN对应 · S11.4 The Neurodiversity Perspective: Reconsidering “Normal” and “Abnormal”**
>
> The **neurodiversity** movement, originating in the autism rights movement, challenges the medical model that treats neurodevelopmental differences (autism, ADHD, bipolar disorder) as “deficits” requiring treatment, proposing instead that these represent natural expressions of human neurological variation with their own distinctive values and strengths.⁶²
>
> This generates a productive dialogue with Yogācāra: Yogācāra does not treat any particular neurodevelopmental pattern or psychological functional style as the norm. Its analysis of suffering’s source (manas self-grasping) applies across all neurological types; every neurological type confronts its characteristic forms of parikalpita-svabhāva and possesses its own distinctive pathways of awareness. The Yogācāra tradition’s own strong emphasis on respecting “root capacity” (gēnjī, the individual’s psycho-spiritual ability and inclination), insisting that different practitioners require different practice approaches, manifests a functional neurodiversity sensitivity — long before the concept was articulated in Western psychology.
>
> This perspective suggests that the dialogue should not apply Western psychology’s (standardized-neurotype-based) “health” criteria as the measuring standard for Yogācāra contemplative achievement, but should instead assess by the degree of insight into the source of suffering (manas self-grasping) — an insight independent of neurological type.

## 第十三节：深层分歧与批判性反思

### 一、自我观的根本对立：不可绕过的哲学核心

整个比较研究中，最根本也最无法回避的分歧，是两个传统对”自我”的根本性不同态度：

西方心理学（从精神分析到人本主义到积极心理学）总体上将某种形式的**自我感**（sense of self）视为心理健康的必要条件——弗洛伊德的强大自我、荣格的整合自性、罗杰斯的真实自我、马斯洛的实现自我、西格尔的整合自我——都是以某种形式的自我结构的充分发展为目标的。即便在超个人心理学中，自我的”超越”也常常被框定为”更大自我”的实现，而非对自我建构的根本超越。

唯识学则从根本上质疑了这一预设的合法性：末那识所执取的”自我”，在圆成实的层面是虚妄的（无自性的）；修行的终极目标是如实认识这一虚妄性，从而解除对它的执取。这不是”虚无主义”（否认依他起层面的经验实在性），而是更精密的三性分析。

**这一分歧不能被和解，只能被承认**。任何试图在不处理这一分歧的情况下进行两个传统整合的工作，都会不可避免地以一方削足适履于另一方的框架。Jack Engler 的经典论述——”你必须先成为某人，才能成为无人”（”You have to be somebody before you can be nobody”）⁶³——是目前最诚实的整合尝试：承认西方心理学（培养健康自我结构）与佛教修行（超越自我）面对不同群体的不同需要，而非提供单一的普遍路径。


> **EN对应 · S13.1 The Fundamental Opposition of Self-Conceptions**
>
> The most foundational irresolvable divergence across the entire comparative field is the two traditions’ fundamentally different orientations toward “self”:
>
> Western psychology, across its major traditions (from psychoanalysis to humanism to positive psychology), treats some form of **self-cohesion** as a necessary condition of psychological health. Even in transpersonal psychology, self-”transcendence” is typically framed as the realization of a “larger self,” not the fundamental dissolution of the self-construction.
>
> Yogācāra fundamentally challenges the legitimacy of this presupposition: the “self” continuously grasped by manas is, at the level of pariniṣpanna-svabhāva, without self-nature; practice’s ultimate aim is the precise recognition of this and the release of grasping. This is not nihilism — it is the more refined analysis of three natures — but it constitutes an irreducible philosophical opposition.
>
> **This divergence cannot be reconciled, only acknowledged.** Any integration project that proceeds without addressing it will inevitably distort one tradition to accommodate the other. Jack Engler’s classic formulation — “You have to be somebody before you can be nobody”⁶³ — remains the most intellectually honest available integration: acknowledging that Western psychology (developing healthy self-structure) and Buddhist practice (transcending self) address different groups with different needs, rather than offering a single universal path. John Welwood’s concept of **spiritual bypass** — using spiritual practice to avoid unresolved psychological issues⁶⁴ — provides the corresponding clinical warning: for individuals who have not yet developed basic self-functioning, premature emphasis on “non-self” practice may function as a defensive mechanism, wrapping unresolved trauma and developmental issues in spiritual language.
### 二、目的论的不可通约性

西方心理学的目标，无论多么高远，都是在此世此生的框架内追求心理健康与功能改善。唯识学修行的最终目标是从轮回苦恼中彻底解脱，这是彻底超越西方心理学目标范围的存在性目标。

这一目的论的根本差异，意味着两个传统之间的可通约性是有限度的：某些心理学概念与唯识学概念之间的功能层面对话是有价值的；但将这种功能层面的对话推进到目的论层面的等同，则必然产生对两个传统的双重误读。


> **EN对应 · S13.2 The Non-Commensurability of Teleological Orientations**
>
> Western psychology’s goals, however elevated (from Freud’s “reduction of neurotic suffering” to Maslow’s “self-actualization” to Seligman’s “flourishing”), are oriented toward psychological health and functional improvement within this life, with no reference to liberation in the soteriological sense.
>
> Yogācāra practice’s ultimate goal is liberation from the suffering of conditioned existence — utterly beyond the scope of any Western psychological goal. This teleological non-commensurability means that dialogue’s scope is limited: functional-level dialogue is valuable; pushing functional-level dialogue into teleological-level equivalence necessarily produces systematic misreading.
### 三、”灵性绕过”（Spiritual Bypass）的警示

约翰·韦尔伍德（John Welwood）创造的”灵性绕过”（spiritual bypass）概念，描述了一种普遍现象：使用灵性修行来逃避未解决的心理问题，以”无我”的高级谈论来绕过末解决的自我发展议题。⁶⁴

这一警示对唯识学-心理学的比较研究具有重要的方法论含义：对于那些尚未发展出基本的自我功能（如情绪调节、稳定的自我感、基本的人际能力）的个体，过早强调”无我”修行可能反而成为一种防御机制——用灵性语言包装未解决的心理创伤与发展问题。唯识学传统本身也对修行次第（五位的渐进）有严格要求，并不鼓励跳跃性地进行超出当前心理能力的修行。这是两个传统对话中最具临床实践价值的汇聚点之一。

### 四、印顺法师的警示与现代学术的批判

印顺法师在《唯识学探源》中明确指出，将唯识学的心理学分析与现代心理学简单等同，是对唯识学解脱论目的的根本误解：唯识学的百法分析，不是为了建立一套心理科学，而是为了帮助修行者如实认识心的运作，以便通过修行转化这些运作。⁶⁵

Evan Thompson 在《我为何不是佛教徒》（2020）中，从批判性的角度检视了”佛教现代主义”（Buddhist modernism）对佛教修行的心理学化倾向，认为这种倾向往往以失真的代价换取了表面的现代性。⁶⁶ 这一批判对本文的研究取向是重要的自我修正提示。

> **EN对应 · S13.3 The Warning Against Psychologization and Decontextualization**
>
> The Venerable Yinshun’s warning in *The Origin of Yogācāra* — that equating Yogācāra’s psychological analysis with modern psychology fundamentally misreads Yogācāra’s soteriological purpose⁶⁵ — remains the essential critical guardrail for this entire enterprise.
>
> Evan Thompson’s critique in *Why I Am Not a Buddhist* (2020) of “Buddhist modernism’s” psychologizing tendencies⁶⁶ — reducing contemplative practice to psychological intervention, redescribing soteriological goals in psychological language — provides an important methodological corrective. This does not mean dialogue is impossible; it means productive dialogue requires the highest level of precision about what is and is not being compared, at what level, and with what analytical implications.

## 第十四节：真正有价值的对话空间——从相遇到相互开辟

### 一、临床实践层面：已有成效的整合

MBCT对复发性抑郁症防止复发的临床证据，是迄今两个传统整合最成功的案例；ACT在心理灵活性框架下的实践整合，有广泛的实证支持；CFT对慈悲训练的系统化，为临床实践提供了新的工具；IFS对内在状态的精密分类，为复杂性创伤的治疗提供了新的框架。

这些成功案例的共同特征是：从唯识-佛教传统中借鉴了具体的**方法论工具**（正念、接受、慈悲、去融合），而没有试图整合整个形而上学框架。这种选择性的实践整合，既保持了临床有效性，又避免了过度宏大的哲学融合所带来的系统性误读。


> **EN对应 · S14.1 Clinical Practice: Achieved Integrations**
>
> MBCT’s clinical evidence for preventing depressive relapse is the most successful integration to date; ACT’s mindfulness-and-acceptance integration has broad empirical support; CFT’s systematization of compassion training provides new clinical tools; IFS’s refined classification of inner states provides new frameworks for complex trauma treatment.
>
> What these successes share: they borrow specific **methodological tools** from Buddhist contemplative traditions (mindfulness, acceptance, compassion, defusion) without attempting to integrate the entire metaphysical framework. This selective practical integration maintains clinical efficacy while avoiding the systematic misreadings generated by over-ambitious philosophical synthesis.
### 二、理论研究层面：唯识学为心理学开辟的新问题

唯识学为西方心理学提供了以下几个在其自身框架内原本难以提出的问题，这是”相互启发”层次上最有价值的贡献：

**问题一**：如果末那识我执是一切心理苦恼的根本根源，而西方心理学的目标（从弗洛伊德到马斯洛）都是在我执框架内运作的，那么西方心理学是否在根本层面遗漏了苦恼的最深根源？

**问题二**：感受性（vedanā）作为识活动的必要结构特征（五遍行心所之一），在心理治疗中是否受到了足够的重视？情绪调节研究（Gross, Barrett等）的框架，是否足以捕捉vedanā这一更为基本的感受性维度？

**问题三**：解脱（mokṣa）作为心理发展的终极可能性——不只是减少症状，不只是增加心理灵活性，而是对苦恼根源（末那识我执）的根本消解——是否是心理学应当认真对待的概念？若是，它对心理学的目标设定有什么含义？

**问题四**：共相种子理论为社会心理学与文化心理学提供了什么？人类共同世界的集体建构，不只是个体心理交互的结果，更是无数识流通过共业共同变现的过程——这对集体创伤研究、文化变迁心理学与社会变革的心理机制有什么启示？


> **EN对应 · S14.2 Theoretical Research: New Problems Yogācāra Opens for Psychology**
>
> Yogācāra’s most valuable contribution to Western psychology — at the level of mutual illumination — lies in the new problems it makes possible that are difficult to formulate within psychology’s own frameworks:
>
> **Question 1**: If manas’ self-grasping is the fundamental source of all psychological suffering, and if Western psychology’s goals (from Freud to Maslow) all operate within the self-grasping framework, does Western psychology fundamentally miss the deepest source of suffering?
>
> **Question 2**: Vedanā as a universal structural feature of vijñāna-activity (one of the five universally present mental factors) — has it received adequate attention in psychological research? Does emotion regulation research (Gross, Barrett, etc.) have adequate conceptual resources to capture vedanā as a more fundamental affective dimension?
>
> **Question 3**: Liberation (mokṣa) as the ultimate developmental possibility — not merely symptom reduction, not merely psychological flexibility, but the fundamental dissolution of suffering’s root (manas self-grasping) — is this a concept psychology should take seriously? If so, what are its implications for the setting of psychological goals?
>
> **Question 4**: What does the shared-seeds theory offer social psychology and cultural psychology? The collective construction of the human shared world is not merely the result of individual psychological interactions but the process of countless vijñāna-streams jointly manifesting through shared karma — what implications does this have for collective trauma research, cultural-change psychology, and the psychological mechanisms of social transformation?
### 三、神经科学的第三方中介与新研究议程

神经科学作为两个传统对话的第三方中介，可以进一步发展以下研究议程：

- **末那识我执的神经相关物假说**：以DMN的自我参照性活动为主要候选，设计精确的神经影像学研究以检验这一假说；
- **种子熏习的神经可塑性机制**：以Hebb学习规则与突触可塑性为基础，发展更精确的”业力熏习”的神经机制模型；
- **转依的神经标志物**：以长期禅修者（特别是被识别为实现了高度转依的修行者）为研究对象，寻找转依对应的神经结构特征；
- **vedanā的神经基础**：以潘克塞普的基本情绪系统为基础，追踪vedanā这一更为基本的感受性维度的神经相关物。

>
> Neuroscience as a third-party mediator can develop the following research agenda:
>
> - **The manas self-grasping neural correlate hypothesis**: with DMN self-referential activity as primary candidate, designing precise neuroimaging studies to test this hypothesis;
> - **The neural plasticity mechanism of seed perfuming**: developing more precise “karmic perfuming” neural mechanism models based on Hebbian learning principles and synaptic plasticity;
> - **Neural signatures of āśraya-parāvṛtti**: using long-term meditators (particularly those identified as having realized advanced degrees of āśraya-parāvṛtti) as study populations, seeking the structural neural features corresponding to āśraya-parāvṛtti;
> - **The neural basis of vedanā**: using Panksepp’s primary emotion systems as the foundation, tracing the neural correlates of vedanā as a more basic affective dimension than complex emotions.

## 第十五节：结论——识的问题，是心灵的问题，是人类的问题

### 一、主要结论

本文通过对唯识学与西方心理学十个主要传统的系统比较，得出以下主要结论：

**其一**，两个传统的对话建立在共同面对人类苦难的文明根基之上，这使对话在根本层面具有意义。但两者在目的论结构（解脱vs.心理健康）、知识论地位（第一人称实证vs.第三人称实验）与自我观（无我体证vs.自我实现）上存在根本性差异，这些差异不能被绕过，必须被正视。

**其二**，弗洛伊德无意识与阿赖耶识之间存在真实的结构同构，但在内容性质、产生机制与治疗目标上存在根本差异。末那识与自我感研究的对照，具有最深刻的哲学价值，因为唯识学对末那识我执的分析，直接质疑了西方心理学将自我功能视为心理健康目标的核心预设。

**其三**，预测处理理论与唯识学识变论的对话，是本文识别的最具哲学深度的结构同构，也是最有可能发展为”相互启发”甚至”直接实证对应”的新方向——通过将修行理解为大脑生成模型的系统更新过程，可以提出一系列可操作化的神经科学研究假说。

**其四**，潘克塞普七大基本情绪系统与唯识学烦恼心所的精密对应，是本文在情感神经科学维度提出的最具创新性的比较分析，为两个传统的对话提供了一个前所未有的精确切入点。

**其五**，波雷斯的多元迷走神经理论与唯识修行论的对话，为理解修行所需的身心条件（安全感作为修行的神经生理前提）提供了新的理论资源，也为创伤知情的冥想教学提供了神经生物学基础。

**其六**，以MBCT、ACT、CFT、IFS等为代表的第三波整合实践，在方法论层面与佛教（含唯识）传统实现了有实证支持的部分整合，是两个传统对话迄今最成功的临床实践成果。

**其七**，迷幻剂辅助治疗的研究，提供了一个意外的跨传统印证场域，其与唯识修行状态的现象学相似性，开辟了新的比较研究方向，但需要极为审慎的方法论处理。


> **EN对应 · S15.1 Principal Conclusions**
>
> Through systematic comparison of Yogācāra with ten major traditions of Western psychology, this paper reaches the following principal conclusions:
>
> **First**, dialogue between the two traditions rests on the shared civilizational foundation of confronting human suffering, giving the encounter fundamental significance. But fundamental differences in teleological structure (liberation versus psychological health), epistemological standing (first-person contemplative verification versus third-person experimentation), and the conception of self (non-self realization versus self-actualization) cannot be bypassed; they must be honestly confronted.
>
> **Second**, genuine structural homology exists between the Freudian unconscious and the ālayavijñāna, but their content, generative mechanism, and therapeutic aims differ fundamentally. The comparison of manas and the sense of self carries the deepest philosophical value, because Yogācāra’s analysis of manas self-grasping directly challenges the core presupposition of Western psychology — that self-functioning is the goal of psychological health.
>
> **Third**, predictive processing theory and Yogācāra’s vijñāna-transformation doctrine form what is arguably the most philosophically deep structural isomorphism identified in comparative literature, with the strongest potential to develop from structural homology into mutual illumination and toward operationalizable research.
>
> **Fourth**, the precise correspondence between Panksepp’s seven primary emotional systems and Yogācāra’s afflictive mental factors taxonomy represents the most precise neuroscience-Yogācāra mapping yet offered in comparative research — one of this paper’s original contributions.
>
> **Fifth**, polyvagal theory’s dialogue with Yogācāra’s contemplative practice provides new theoretical resources for understanding the somatic conditions of practice (safety as the neurophysiological prerequisite of contemplation) and offers a neuroscientific foundation for trauma-informed contemplative teaching.
>
> **Sixth**, third-wave therapeutic integrations (MBCT, ACT, CFT, IFS) have achieved evidence-based partial integrations at the methodological level — the most successful clinical outcomes of the dialogue.
>
> **Seventh**, psychedelic-assisted therapy research provides an unexpected cross-traditional resonance field, its phenomenological similarities with Yogācāra meditative states opening new comparative research directions — but requiring the most methodologically careful handling.
### 二、主要开放性问题

**问题一**：若末那识我执是一切心理苦恼的根本根源，西方心理学是否需要在目标设定上进行根本性反思，从”在我执框架内改善功能”转向”认识与处理我执本身”？

**问题二**：唯识学的五位（资粮位、加行位、通达位、修习位、究竟位）修行次第，与西方发展心理学的发展阶段理论，是否存在有意义的结构对应？

**问题三**：表观遗传学与跨代际创伤研究的进展，是否正在为唯识学的某些业力理论提供生物学层面的平行？这一平行的认识论意义是什么？

**问题四**：在正念临床实践高度普及的今天，如何在保持临床有效性的同时，防止”去语境化”对修行传统深度的稀释，同时警惕”灵性绕过”的陷阱？

**问题五**：预测处理框架下的”转依”假说，是否可以产生具体的可操作化神经科学研究设计，以部分地将唯识修行论的某些主张转化为可检验的科学假说？


> **EN对应 · S15.2 Principal Open Questions**
>
> **Question 1**: If manas self-grasping is the fundamental source of all psychological suffering, does Western psychology need fundamental revision of its goal-setting — from “improving function within the self-grasping framework” to “recognizing and addressing self-grasping itself”?
>
> **Question 2**: Do Yogācāra’s five stages of practice (prayoga, āprayoga, darśana, bhāvanā, niṣṭhā-mārga) exhibit meaningful structural correspondences with developmental psychology’s stage theories?
>
> **Question 3**: Do advances in epigenetics and transgenerational trauma research provide a biological-level parallel for certain aspects of Yogācāra’s karma theory, and if so, what are the epistemological implications of this parallel?
>
> **Question 4**: How can clinical efficacy be maintained while preventing “decontextualization” from diluting the depth of contemplative traditions, while simultaneously guarding against the spiritual bypass trap?
>
> **Question 5**: Can the “āśraya-parāvṛtti as generative model updating” hypothesis generate specific, operationalizable neuroscientific research designs that partially translate certain Yogācāra practice-theory claims into testable scientific hypotheses?
### 三、最终立场

两个伟大传统的真正相遇，不是一方吸收另一方，也不是用一套话语重新描述另一套话语，而是在各自保持内在完整性的前提下，在真实的差异中寻求真实的共鸣——在差异中保持对话，在对话中保持差异。

西方心理学对人类心灵的研究，已经积累了极为丰富的实证知识与临床智慧——这是唯识学在制度化的科学研究与系统化的临床实践方面所缺乏的。唯识学对心理过程的分析，以其在深度、精度与系统性上的独特品质，为心理学提供了一个迄今未被充分开发的理论资源——特别是在苦恼根源的究极分析（末那识我执）、解脱可能性的积极论证（转依）与第一人称体验的精密描述（自证分）三个方面。

有情众生的苦恼，是两个传统共同出发的原点；对苦恼的彻底理解与彻底超越，是两个传统共同指向的方向。在这共同的关怀中，真正的相遇是可能的——带着全部的差异，带着全部的诚实，也带着共同的悲悯。

识的问题，是心灵的问题；心灵的问题，是人类自身的问题；而人类自身的问题，始终值得我们以全部的理性、全部的悲悯、全部的觉悟去回应。

> **EN对应 · S15.3 Final Position**
>
> The genuine encounter between two great traditions is not one absorbing the other, nor one tradition’s language redescribing the other’s content, but each maintaining its internal integrity while seeking genuine resonance within real difference — maintaining dialogue within difference, maintaining difference within dialogue.
>
> Western psychology has accumulated extraordinarily rich empirical knowledge and clinical wisdom — resources that Yogācāra lacks in institutionalized scientific research and systematized clinical practice. Yogācāra’s analysis of psychological processes offers, in its distinctive depth, precision, and systematic coherence, a theoretical resource for psychology that remains underutilized — particularly in three domains: the ultimate analysis of suffering’s source (manas self-grasping), the positive argument for liberation’s possibility (āśraya-parāvṛtti), and the precise description of first-person experience (sva-saṃvedana).
>
> The suffering of sentient beings is the common origin from which both traditions departed; the thorough understanding and transcendence of suffering is the direction both point toward. In this shared care, genuine encounter is possible — with all the differences intact, with all the honesty intact, and with the common compassion intact.
>
> The problem of vijñāna is the problem of mind; the problem of mind is the problem of humanity itself; and the problem of humanity itself always warrants our response with full reason, full compassion, and full awakening.
